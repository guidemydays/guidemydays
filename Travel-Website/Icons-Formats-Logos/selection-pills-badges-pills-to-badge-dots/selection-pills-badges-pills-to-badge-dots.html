Brain / Reference / Formatting

These are the only pills, badges & dots approved to use on the site — AND HOVER

Rules

1
Always reach for the lighter colors first — they're easier to read — and bring in the rest only once a group needs more selections than the lighter set covers. A pale fill with dark ink (most of Family 1 — Calm Pastel's own lighter members: Light Grey, Blue, Honey, White Slate, White Gold, Fog, Amber, Bronze, Rust, Saddle) reads more comfortably at a glance than a deeper or fully-saturated fill — start every new group there. Pull in a deeper Calm Pastel tone, or move to Family 2 — Solid, only when the group genuinely needs more distinct members than the lighter set can supply. When that happens, pick the most different colors from each other first — not simply the next ones in the gallery — so the newly-added members are as far apart, perceptually, as the rest of the group; two similar-reading tones sitting side by side defeats the point of adding more.
2
Guides are exempted — this doc never governs a guide page. A guide page (Travel-Website/guides/{city_slug}.html) and its Read-About/stops-map pair run their own separate rule set end to end — assets/guide-style.css, Guide Structure.html, Stops Structure.html, and the rest of Brain/CORE RULES/. A pill, badge, or dot that happens to appear inside a guide is governed by the guide's own doc set, never by this one.
3
No two pills in the same row/group share a color. Every sibling in one filter/legend/badge row is a different color, no exceptions — two different meanings reading as the same pill is drift, full stop. If a row is running out of distinct colors, that's a signal to re-check the grouping, never a reason to repeat one.
4
An icon-bearing pill needs more vertical room than the plain-text reference — use the shared has-icon modifier (padding:7px 14px), never a page-local override. web-travel-style.css's --fam-pad (4px 9px) is sized for text alone; an icon taller than the text line reads squished at that padding. has-icon isn't an arbitrary bump — measured live, the plain reference renders 130×22px (a 5.91:1 width:height ratio) and the has-icon version renders 188×30px (6.32:1) on the same "Very expensive" label — nearly the same proportion, just scaled up for the icon, not a different shape. Every pill in the same group takes the size of its tallest member — but "same group" means pills that share one purpose (e.g. the Cheap→Very-expensive cost-tier scale), not just visual adjacency in a row. A standalone control with a different job (an "All"/reset pill, say) sitting next to that group stays its own size even if it never carries an icon itself.
5
No pill ever defaults to an uncontrolled white — and "every pill in the group shares one color" is the Selection Pill exception, not a default. A pill whose members carry distinct meanings (different regions, categories, statuses) needs a real .cp-*/.sb-* fill on every member — a colored dot icon next to plain uncontrolled white is not colored, it's undressed. Where a genuinely neutral tone is needed (an "All" pill, or a pill that truly carries no category meaning), pick one of Family 1's own neutral-reading tones (Light Grey, Fog, Grey, White Slate, White Gold — § below) — deliberately, by name, never as an unstyled fallback. An "All"/reset pill takes the deepest tone in the family — it should read as the darker pill next to the group it resets, not the same weight or lighter. "Every pill shares one color" is only correct for a true Selection Pill — options that are genuinely interchangeable except for which one is picked (a month, a preset) — never for a group whose members mean different things. Filter-selection controls (index.html's Help Me Choose, .fnd-ctl — a dropdown/select styled as a pill: "Flexible," "No preference," an emoji icon, white/cream at rest) are a different component category, not a Badge/Pill-to-Badge/Selection-Pill, and can vary outside this format — they're form inputs wearing the site's search-bar skin, not category badges, so white-at-rest and one uniform look across all of them is correct there, not a violation of this rule.
6
One pill group, one color family — .cp-* and .sb-* never mix within the same group. If a group's natural color needs a shade the current family lacks (there is no sb-purple, for instance), the fix is to pick a different color that family does have, or move every member of that group onto another family together — never leave one member on .cp-* and its siblings on .sb-*. Family choice is a per-group decision, made once, applied to every member.
7
Interaction is always one of the 5 approved variants — hov-darken, hov-lift, hov-press, hov-glow, or the focus ring (demoed below) — never a hand-rolled effect. All five derive their color from the pill's own family (var(--sp-rgb)/var(--sp-ink)), so a correctly-implemented one stays consistent with the pill's own palette. A page-local box-shadow glow, a hardcoded brightness tint, or any hover that doesn't map to one of the four hover variants — or a keyboard-focus indicator that isn't the shared outline ring — is drift, however deliberate it looks. The ring is the odd one out on purpose: it fires on :focus-visible, not :hover (§ Ring Hover explains why), which is why it's counted separately from the other four rather than folded into them.
8
Every pill/badge's text is centered both horizontally and vertically — never left-, right-, top-, or bottom-anchored inside its own box. align-items:center alone is not enough: an inline-flex box with no justify-content:center defaults to flex-start, so the moment the box is stretched wider than its text (a grid cell sized to 1fr, a shared min-width equalization pass), the label drifts to the left edge and only looks centered by accident when the box happens to hug the text exactly. Caught live on .selection-pill itself (owner: "these are not even centered") — the shared base rule was missing justify-content:center, silently left-aligning every stretched instance sitewide, from a plain hover fix like this one to a full month-grid migration. Both properties, every time: align-items:center AND justify-content:center.
9
Pills that are the same control — siblings in one filter row, tab strip, or legend — share ONE width: the widest label's own natural width, not each pill sized to just its own text. CSS Grid's 1fr auto-columns on an intrinsically-sized track does NOT equalize to the widest item in practice (verified empirically, not a working trick) — each column still sizes to its own content, so measuring the widest sibling's rendered width in JS (getBoundingClientRect) and setting that width on every member of the group is the reliable fix. The one exception is a pill labeled "All" (or any reset/all-encompassing option): it is a different control from the group it sits beside (Rule 4's own-size carve-out above), so it is never stretched to the group's shared width — it wraps to its own content instead.
10
Calm Pastel (.cp-*) is the main palette for selection pills — reach for it first, and it can be used anywhere. Solid (.sb-*) is secondary: reserve it for a standalone label or badge on its own, or for the times a context genuinely needs a second, visually distinct group of colors alongside an existing Calm Pastel group (e.g. two independent legends on the same page that must not be confused with each other). When only one family is needed, that's Calm Pastel — Solid is not an equal alternative to reach for by default.
11
Case is a per-component rule, not a per-family one — a Selection Pill's label is never all-caps; a Badge's or Pill-to-Badge's always is. .selection-pill carries no text-transform at all (sentence case, whatever the label was typed as) — this is the jump-nav chip, the filter/legend pill, the "one choice marked selected" control shown throughout this doc. .badge and .pillbadge both set text-transform:uppercase (owner rule 2026-08-20) — same box, same colour mechanism, case included; behaviour and size are the only things that tell them apart from each other. Swapping a Selection Pill to caps, or a Badge/Pill-to-Badge to sentence case, is a component mismatch even if the color and shape are otherwise correct.
12
Selected is a background blend only — never a border, an outline, or a checkmark. .chk-toggle.is-sel mixes the tone's own wash toward its own ink (color-mix(in srgb, var(--wash) 58%, var(--ink) 42%) for Calm Pastel/neutral-reading tones, color-mix(in srgb, var(--wash) 65%, black 35%) for Solid) — the border stays exactly what it was at Rest, and no checkmark ships (a hand-drawn placeholder shipped briefly on 2026-08-31 and was pulled the same day: every check-shaped specimen in Site-Icons.html is a fixed-color decorative badge, none inherit a pill's own ink, so none was usable — logged to Open Recommendations as a catalogue gap). The ring belongs to Focus alone — Ring Hover's own Selected column proves this by showing a ring with zero fill change (click any pill there: the color never moves, only the ring appears), and elsewhere Selected never gains an outline/box-shadow ring no matter which family or which other mechanic is also being demoed on the same row. filter:brightness() is never used for Selected's fill — it darkens the ink-mixed text along with the background (verified live: a Solid tone's white label read visibly dimmer under filter even though its computed color stayed #fff), which color-mix() on background alone does not.
13
No pill, badge, dot, hover effect, or search styling is ever hardcoded — no matter what, and none of them ever falls back to a plain unstyled format either. Every color, border, background, spacing, and hover value on a pill, badge, dot, or search control must use a CSS custom property (var(--…)) and the shared classes/hover variants (§ above) — never a literal hex value, pixel count, named color, or a hand-rolled hover effect. A hardcoded value bypasses the theme and the design token system, making the style invisible to dark mode and impossible to update globally. Equally, if the shared stylesheet or script fails to load, a page never renders a degraded, unstyled pill or search box as a fallback — there is no plain-HTML backup appearance; the shared CSS/JS is a hard dependency, not a progressive enhancement. When a token for a value doesn't exist yet, create the token — don't inline the value, and don't ship a fallback in its place.
14
Every new page, edit, or addition that touches a pill or search control must cross-check against this doc and TVESearch.html before shipping. This doc is the single source of truth for pill and badge styling. TVESearch.html is the single source of truth for search bar dimensions, states, and wiring. A change that looks correct in isolation may still conflict with the approved spec — the cross-check is what catches that. Reading the spec is the last step before committing, not an optional review.

How to apply on a real page: drop the doc's demo-only 2 — the real classes are hov-darken, hov-lift, hov-press, hov-glow (Rule 7). For any tone already defined in web-travel-style.css (its own .cp-* / .sb-* class), --sp-ink / --sp-rgb / --sp-wash are supplied automatically by that class — never set --hv-ink / --hv-rgb / --hv-wash by hand on a live page, that trio only exists to run this doc's own demo widgets. Solid family needs a stronger push than Calm Pastel (2026-09-01): the same mechanic that reads clearly on a pale wash is too subtle on an already-saturated fill, so Darken/Lift/Glow/Press each carry a second, Solid-only rule (in this doc: hov2-darken-solid, hov2-lift-solid, hov2-glow-solid, hov2-press-solid/hov2-press-active-solid) layered on top of the shared one, using a bigger percentage/brightness value on the exact same property — apply the same doubling on a real page (e.g. a stronger color-mix() or filter value gated to .sb-*). Every demo span needs its own border — a tone whose wash is pure white (White Slate, White Gold) has nothing else to render its shape against the page; the border is not decorative here, it's the only thing keeping the pill visible. Selected in production is still .is-on (outline); the color-mix wash-to-ink blend shown in the sections below (no checkmark, no border change — see Rule 12) is this doc's current researched recommendation, not yet shipped anywhere. Note: several tones in the Family 1 gallery above (Rust included) don't have a matching .cp-* rule in web-travel-style.css yet — those need adding to production CSS before a crib can wire them up by class name alone.

Researched, not guessed: Press active state corrected from brightness(.85) to brightness(.95) — same pattern as Darken: the old 15% drop read heavier than convention. Design-system practice (LogRocket, Figma, NN/g, GeeksforGeeks) points to a light brightness dip (~5%) paired with scale(.98) and an inset shadow to read as "pressing in," registering within 100–150ms. scale(.98) and the doc's existing .15s transition already matched; only the brightness value needed correcting. Middle column is genuinely interactive (2026-09-01): it used to be a frozen preview with a hardcoded filter, so real hovering did nothing — the hov2-press class now sits on the Hover column itself, and Active moved to its own class (hov2-press-active) with a click-to-preview toggle, since :active only shows while the mouse is physically held down. Hover is color-mix(), not filter:brightness(): brightening an already-near-white wash (Honey, Amber, White Slate…) clips straight to invisible white, so Hover mixes in a fixed 12% white instead — proportional for every tone, and paired with a border-color change and a real border on every span (some tones have no other way to render their shape against the page). Solid needs a stronger push on both columns for the same reason as Darken/Lift/Glow — hov2-press-solid mixes in 30% white instead of 12%, and hov2-press-active-solid darkens to brightness(.8) instead of .95.

Researched, not guessed: the outline ring is a focus indicator elsewhere — "you can activate this now via keyboard" — not a selected one. Focus fires on :focus-visible (keyboard nav only) and is accessibility-critical (WCAG 2.4.13 requires 3:1 contrast against both the focused and unfocused states); selected is a persistent condition. WCAG also says not to hardcode one fixed ring colour everywhere — "focus is a state, not a colour" — since a single brand colour can fail contrast depending on background. outline:3px solid currentColor already satisfies that: it adapts to each pill's own ink automatically. This site's old .is-on ring used exactly this mechanic but to mean Selected — relabeled here to mean Focus instead, order fixed to Rest → Focus → Selected (2026-09-01, matching the Hover-in-the-middle column order every other section uses). Selected here shows a ring and nothing else (Rule 12): click any pill in the Selected column — the fill never changes, only a ring appears, in the tone's own color. This is a deliberate exception to "the ring belongs to Focus alone": a real Selected pill can also receive keyboard focus, and a ring is the only way to show that combined state without also re-demonstrating Selected's own background blend (already shown in every other section). --ink means "text color" on a Solid tone (white), not "the tone's identity" — so Solid's ring reads var(--wash) instead (the fill color itself) via a dedicated override, or it renders as an invisible white ring on a light page.

Give the ring room, or it collides with its neighbor — in every direction, not just sideways. outline:3px solid; outline-offset:3px draws 6px past the pill's own border on every side — in a row with the default small gap, one pill's focus ring touches or overlaps the next pill's box (this is exactly what happened in the Shape reference row above, before its gap was widened), and the same collision happens vertically: a table of rows (like the Rest/Hover/Selected grids above) needs the SAME wider gap between one row and the row below it, not just between columns — an 8px row-gap lets one row's ring touch the pill sitting directly underneath it. Since Rule 7 requires the shared outline ring as the keyboard-focus indicator on every pill, every row AND every row-to-row gap needs at least 16–20px, not the tighter 4–8px that's fine for a row that only needs to worry about Darken/Lift/Glow/Press.
