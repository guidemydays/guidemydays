Airport Fast Track & Priority Security

Priority security exists at some airports — and it isn't the TSA line.

A separate paid, card-linked, or status-based lane before the X-ray belt, at airports large enough to run one. This covers the airport's or an airline's own fast-track service — not US TSA PreCheck or CLEAR (see Trusted Traveler Programs), and not what happens once you're through the checkpoint (see Lounges). Only airports with a genuine, officially documented service are listed — a handful of well-marketed "fast track" names turned out to be private concierge resellers with no actual separate lane, and those are left off. Few of these programs publish an exact time saved; expect a shorter line, not a guaranteed number.

Europe
LHR London Heathrow Fast Track Departures/Arrivals — Heathrow's own paid lane, bookable in one-hour windows, all terminals. Free for some premium-cabin and elite-status tickets; Heathrow says to confirm with your airline. heathrow.com › PaidStatus tier
CDG Paris Charles de Gaulle SkyPriority — Air France/SkyTeam's own branded security lane. Free for Flying Blue Silver and above, SkyTeam Elite/Elite Plus, or Business/First cabin. Unlike most airports here, there's no general paid option — status or cabin is the only way in. reporting on SkyPriority › Status tier
FRA Frankfurt Frankfurt Airport FRA SmartWay — Frankfurt Airport's own free, book-ahead lane (Terminal 1, Areas A & B, and Terminal 3), open to anyone regardless of status, up to 72 hours ahead, groups of up to 5. Separately, Lufthansa runs its own free Security Fast Lanes for HON Circle, Senator, Frequent Traveller, Star Alliance Gold, and First/Business Class. frankfurt-airport.com › Free to bookStatus tier
AMS Amsterdam Schiphol Privium — Schiphol's own paid membership club (Basic €180/year, Extra €235/year), open to anyone, priority security at every checkpoint. Business/First cabin and top-tier SkyTeam/Flying Blue elites get a priority lane free. schiphol.nl › PaidStatus tier
MAD Madrid Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Fast Track — Aena's own paid lane, Terminals T1/T2/T4. Price is dynamic on Aena's own booking page — check live pricing rather than a fixed figure. Free with some Business-class tickets or airline loyalty cards; confirm with your airline. Children 5 and under go free with a paying adult. aena.es › PaidStatus tier
BCN Barcelona Josep Tarradellas Barcelona–El Prat Fast Track — Aena's own paid lane, Terminal T1 only, 04:00–23:00. Same Aena system as Madrid: check live pricing on Aena's site rather than a fixed figure. Free with some Business-class tickets or loyalty cards. aena.es › PaidStatus tier
FCO Rome Fiumicino Fast Track — Aeroporti di Roma's own paid lane, €10 per person (children 0–12 free with a paying adult), Terminal 1 and Terminal 3 East only, 05:00–23:00. Security only — doesn't include passport control, boarding priority, or a lounge. adr.it › Paid
IST Istanbul Istanbul Airport iGA Fast Track — İGA's own paid pass, covering domestic departure, international departure, and international arrival. Reported prices run roughly €27 for fast-track alone up to higher day-pass and annual tiers — treat as approximate and confirm live. Also free (2 uses/6 months) with premium Visa cards (Platinum, Signature, Infinite) and through a Mastercard/DragonPass partnership. igapass.com › PaidCredit card
ZRH Zurich Zurich Airport ZRH Comfort / ZRH Club — the airport's own paid fast-track-plus-lounge product, CHF 49 per person, open to any airline or class. A separate free lane exists for First/Business, HON Circle, Senator, and Star Alliance Gold. flughafen-zuerich.ch › PaidStatus tier
Middle East & Africa
DXB Dubai Dubai International Marhaba Fast Track — Dubai Airports' own meet-and-assist brand, from roughly $28.59 per person for the starter tier up to $300+ for family/Elite tiers. Open to anyone; separate from Business/First lanes many airlines provide directly. marhabaservices.com › Paid
DOH Doha Hamad International Al Maha Services — Qatar Airways' own premium-passenger service, with dedicated security/immigration lanes free for Privilege Club Platinum/Gold and Business/First cabin. A separately purchased fast-track/transit-lounge option exists too — reported around QAR 326–451 per person, treat as approximate. qatarairways.com › Status tierPaid
AUH Abu Dhabi Zayed International Fast Track Services — the airport's own official bookable product for departure, arrival, or transit. Price isn't published outside the live booking flow — check it there rather than a figure quoted here. zayedinternationalairport.ae › Paid
CAI Cairo Cairo International Ahlan — the state airport operator's (EHCAAN) own VIP meet-and-assist brand, distinct from the many unaffiliated private VIP resellers using similar names. Reported around $230 per person for foreign passport holders, $85 for Egyptian passport holders — unconfirmed from an official price page, so treat as approximate. EHCAAN (official) › Paid
North America
JFK New York John F. Kennedy International, Terminal 1 VIP One — a genuinely separate, private TSA checkpoint, not just a faster line at the main one. Silver $45/person, Gold $100, Platinum $200 (Gold/Platinum need 48-hour advance booking). Free for most Priority Pass members (itself bundled with cards like Amex Platinum, Chase Sapphire Reserve, Capital One Venture X), and for First Class on Air France, Korean Air, or Lufthansa. flyvipone.com › PaidCredit cardStatus tier
LAX Los Angeles Los Angeles International American PriorityAAccess — free for AAdvantage Executive Platinum/Platinum/Gold, oneworld Emerald/Sapphire/Ruby, or First/Business cabin. Separately, LAX Fast Lane is the airport's own free reservation system (Terminals 7/8, 5am–1pm) — open to any traveler regardless of status, no purchase or elite tier required. lawa.org › Status tierFree to book
ORD Chicago O'Hare International American PriorityAAccess — free, same AAdvantage/oneworld/cabin criteria as Los Angeles. American also sells Five Star Service, a full escorted concierge including priority security: $350 for the first passenger, $100 per additional adult, $50 per child — must hold a First or Business ticket to purchase. aa.com › Status tierPaid
ATL Atlanta Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Delta Sky Priority — free for Diamond/Platinum/Gold Medallion, Delta One/Premium Select/First, or SkyTeam Elite Plus. Delta's own page describes the security-lane benefit as available at "select airports" without naming Atlanta specifically; its presence here is corroborated by traveler reports rather than an Atlanta-specific line from Delta. delta.com › Status tier
MIA Miami Miami International American PriorityAAccess — free for AAdvantage Executive Platinum/Platinum/Gold, oneworld Emerald/Sapphire/Ruby, or First/Business cabin. Miami is named explicitly in American's own PriorityAAccess launch materials and has lanes at four checkpoints, the widest footprint of any American hub. aa.com › Status tier
SFO San Francisco San Francisco International American PriorityAAccess (free, same criteria as above) and United Premier Access — free for Premier Silver/Gold/Platinum/1K, United Club members, or eligible co-branded cards, and otherwise purchasable per segment. united.com › Status tierCredit cardPaid
YYZ Toronto Toronto Pearson International, Terminals 1 & 3 American Express Priority Security Lane Access — a free, single-use QR-code eGate for Amex Platinum Card (Canada) holders, plus accompanying travelers on the same itinerary. Booked through Amex's own site, not a third-party. americanexpress.com › Credit card
Latin America
GRU São Paulo Guarulhos International Visa Infinite Fast Pass (Terminal 3, 3am–11pm) and W Fast Premium (Terminal 1, 6am–10pm) — two named security lanes confirmed on the airport's own services page, each tied to a specific bank card program rather than open purchase. Security only, not passport control. gru.com.br › Credit card
EZE Buenos Aires Ezeiza International Fast Pass — sold directly by the airport operator (Aeropuertos Argentina 2000), $20 USD per person, covering both migration and security lines. Free or discounted for Mastercard Black cardholders through the Mastercard Airport Experiences app, booked 24 hours ahead. aeropuertosargentina.com › PaidCredit card
Asia-Pacific
HND Tokyo Haneda, Terminal 3 Separate JAL and ANA priority security lanes — each airline's own, not a shared airport product, and strict: JAL requires First Class or JAL Diamond/Global Club Premier status, ANA requires First Class or ANA Diamond status. Business Class alone does not qualify, and other airlines' elite status is not accepted. reporting on JAL/ANA eligibility › Status tier
PEK Beijing Beijing Capital International A named, purchasable frequent-traveler lane sold through Air China's own frequent-flyer store and through DragonPass, Terminals T2/T3. Price could not be confirmed from the pages reachable for this page — check the live Air China or DragonPass listing. A separate, more restricted lane exists for diplomats and APEC card holders — the two aren't the same thing. Air China FFP store › Paid
PVG Shanghai Pudong International Numbered priority security channels (24/25 in China Eastern's own airport-info page) for First/Business cabin and SkyTeam Elite Plus passengers. This is China Eastern's own published lane, not an airport-wide product. ceair.com › Status tier
SIN Singapore Changi, Terminal 3 Singapore Airlines began trialling priority security screening at select gates in May 2026, for Suites/First/Solitaire PPS Club (its top elite tier) only — not yet confirmed as a permanent program. This is separate from Changi's "Premium Check-In," which covers check-in, not security. reporting on the SIA trial › Status tier
SYD Sydney Sydney Airport, Terminal 1 International Fast Track — Sydney Airport's own paid lane (launched July 2026), AU$20 per person, open to any traveler regardless of airline or class, bookable online or on the day. A separate free Priority Lane is First/Business Class only. Sydney Airport's own page is explicit that Fast Track buys queue position, not a guaranteed faster screening time. sydneyairport.com.au › PaidStatus tier
