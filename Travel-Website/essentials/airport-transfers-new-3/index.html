Airport-to-City Transfers

A dedicated express train is the exception. Most airports hand you off to the regular metro instead.

For every major airport across the fleet, the fastest and cheapest ways from the terminal into the city center — the rail line (name, cost, journey time, frequency), the typical rideshare fare, and the public bus. Fills the gap between landing and a guide's Day 1 starting point. For transit once you're already in the city, see Transit Cards; for connecting between flights, see Airport Connections; for a private car booked ahead, see Private Transfers.

27 major airports across the fleet, grouped by region. Every fare, timing and frequency below is sourced from the airport, rail operator or transit authority's own page where one exists — figures marked "varies with demand" reflect dynamic rideshare pricing that has no fixed official rate.

About 50-65 minutes to Midtown via E/J/Z from Jamaica Station, or 60-75 minutes via the A train from Howard Beach. AirTrain runs about every 4-8 minutes; connecting subway trains roughly every 6-10 minutes at peak, less often off-peak. Rideshare Uber — Typically $45-$115 one-way depending on tier and traffic; Uber's own JFK-to-Midtown West estimate averages $112. A $3.50 Port Authority ride-hail pickup fee is automatically added, plus likely tolls (~$9.50 Queens-Midtown Tunnel) and NYC congestion pricing (~$9) depending on route into Manhattan. 50-70 minutes, longer in heavy traffic. Bus GO Airlink NYC coach — $25 one-way on the scheduled Grand Central Express coach; the shared-ride door-to-door shuttle van runs about $35 per person. About 60-90 minutes depending on traffic; Grand Central Express departs hourly, roughly 8am-9pm daily. Regular rail

About 30-45 minutes to Union Station's Patsaouras Transit Plaza, with buses roughly every 30 minutes. Regular rail
