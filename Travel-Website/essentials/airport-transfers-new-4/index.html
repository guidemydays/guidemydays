Airport-to-City Transfers

A dedicated express train is the exception. Most airports hand you off to the regular metro instead.

For every major airport across the fleet, the fastest and cheapest ways from the terminal into the city center — the rail line (name, cost, journey time, frequency), the typical rideshare fare, and the public bus. Fills the gap between landing and a guide's Day 1 starting point.

27 major airports across the fleet, grouped by region. Every fare, timing and frequency below is sourced from the airport, rail operator or transit authority's own page where one exists — figures marked "varies with demand" reflect dynamic rideshare pricing that has no fixed official rate.

Rail

RTA bus route C01 — AED 3–8.50 one-way depending on number of Nol fare zones crossed (Silver Nol card); no direct single bus line runs straight to Downtown Dubai — most riders transfer to the Metro. Roughly 45–65 minutes end-to-end on route C01 to the Bur Dubai/Al Satwa area, longer in traffic.

North America
JFK New YorkJohn F. Kennedy International Regular rail
Rail

AirTrain JFK + subway — $8.75 AirTrain fare + $3.00 subway base fare = about $11.75 one-way total. About 50-65 minutes to Midtown via E/J/Z from Jamaica Station, or 60-75 minutes via the A train from Howard Beach. AirTrain runs about every 4-8 minutes; connecting subway trains roughly every 6-10 minutes at peak, less often off-peak.

Rideshare

Uber — Typically $45-$115 one-way depending on tier and traffic; Uber's own JFK-to-Midtown West estimate averages $112. A $3.50 Port Authority ride-hail pickup fee is automatically added, plus likely tolls (~$9.50 Queens-Midtown Tunnel) and NYC congestion pricing (~$9) depending on route into Manhattan. 50-70 minutes, longer in heavy traffic.

Bus

GO Airlink NYC coach — $25 one-way on the scheduled Grand Central Express coach; the shared-ride door-to-door shuttle van runs about $35 per person. About 60-90 minutes depending on traffic; Grand Central Express departs hourly, roughly 8am-9pm daily.

LAX Los AngelesLos Angeles International Regular rail
Rail

Metro Rail C Line — $1.75 one-way Metro fare (TAP card/contactless), with free transfers within 2 hours; the connecting shuttle bus is free. Roughly 10 minutes on the free shuttle to the transit center, then about 50 minutes total via the C and A Lines to reach Union Station/downtown. Free shuttle every 5-10 minutes at peak (12-20 minutes off-peak); C Line trains roughly every 10-20 minutes.

Rideshare

Uber — Typically $54-$116 one-way depending on vehicle tier, averaging about $58-$60 for standard UberX. LAX adds a $4.00 rideshare access fee per trip at the LAX-it pickup lot (this is due to rise to $6, plus up to $6 more for Central Terminal Area pickups, but only once SkyLink opens, which had not happened as of September 2026). About 30-40 minutes, longer in heavy traffic (official Uber estimate: 34 minutes).

Bus

LAX FlyAway — $12.75 one-way (raised from $9.75 in April 2025). About 30-45 minutes to Union Station's Patsaouras Transit Plaza, with buses roughly every 30 minutes.

Rail

CTA Blue Line — $5.00 one-way when boarding at O'Hare (an airport-originating surcharge on top of the standard $2.50 CTA fare; the surcharge doesn't apply if riding on a day pass). About 40-45 minutes to downtown/the Loop. Departs 24-hour service; about every 3-8 minutes at weekday peak, 7-10 minutes midday/evening, 15-30 minutes overnight.

Rideshare

Bus

GO Airport Express shuttle — About $24 one-way for the shared-ride shuttle to/from downtown Chicago. Roughly 45-75 minutes depending on stops and traffic; shuttles depart about every 10-15 minutes from each terminal.
