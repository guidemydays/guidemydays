Average Costs

Cheap to very expensive, place by place.

Every destination is tagged by its cost of living — what the everyday things cost there: a roof, eating out, the weekly shop, getting around. It is what the place costs, not what a trip costs; the same city takes a careful week or an extravagant one.

Every place, on one axis

One dot per destination, positioned by its cost-of-living index. Height is crowding, not value — where the swarm piles up is where most of the world sits. Click a dot to open it; click a second to compare.
