Carry-on dimensions and checked-bag fees for 40+ airlines across six regions — North America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia, Oceania and Latin America. What fits in the cabin, what the first and second checked bag cost on each fare class, the weight and size at which overweight and oversize charges start, and who gets bags free through elite status or a co-branded credit card. Browse by region below, or search any airline by name.

The spread is wide: Southwest and JetBlue still give most fares a free first checked bag, while Frontier, Wizz Air and other budget carriers charge for any bag bigger than a small under-seat personal item. Emirates, Qatar and Turkish Airlines check bags by weight allowance rather than a flat per-bag fee. Search below for your airline's exact numbers.

Did you know

Bag fees pay no federal ticket tax

Airfare carries a 7.5% federal excise tax — but checked-bag fees are legally exempt from it (26 CFR §49.4261-8), which is part of why they keep climbing even when ticket prices hold steady.
