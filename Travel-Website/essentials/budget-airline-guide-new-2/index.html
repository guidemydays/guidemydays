Know which airport you're actually flying into

3 cities

LONLondon — Stansted, Luton or Southend vs. Heathrow

Stansted (STN)~30–40 miles NE of central London
Stansted Express train, 47 min, from £9.90 booked ahead — or National Express coach from £7 (slower).
Luton (LTN)~35 miles N
Luton DART shuttle (4 min, £4.90) to Luton Airport Parkway, then Thameslink to St Pancras (~30 min, £15.10–£19.50) — two separate fares.
Southend (SEN)~40 miles E
Greater Anglia train to Liverpool Street, 52–58 min, from about £13.40.
Heathrow (LHR) — for comparison~15 miles W
Elizabeth Line ~28–40 min (£15.50 flat), Heathrow Express 15 min (from £10 booked ahead), or Piccadilly line ~50 min (£5.90 contactless — the cheapest option).
Ryanair, easyJet and Wizz Air all fly some London routes from Stansted, Luton or Southend rather than Heathrow — check which airport your fare actually lands at before booking a hotel or onward transport.

PARParis — Beauvais vs. Charles de Gaulle

Beauvais (BVA)~85km N of Paris — genuinely remote
Official shuttle bus to Porte Maillot, 75–90 min, about €17 one-way. Book online in advance; walk-up buses can sell out around peak flight banks.
Charles de Gaulle (CDG) — for comparison~25km NE
RER B train, about 40–50 min to central stations, roughly €12–14 one-way.
Ryanair and Wizz Air's "Paris" routes commonly mean Beauvais, not CDG — the shuttle bus alone can take longer than some of the flights themselves.

FRAFrankfurt — Hahn vs. Frankfurt Main

Frankfurt-Hahn (HHN)~115–125km W — not the same city in any practical sense
FlixBus/Flibco shuttle, about 2h10, roughly €17–20 one-way (cheaper booked online than bought from the driver).
Frankfurt Main (FRA) — for comparison~12km SW
S-Bahn (S8/S9) directly to the city center, about 11–20 min, roughly €5.35–€6.90.
"Frankfurt-Hahn" is the single biggest name-trap in budget-airline booking — it isn't Frankfurt's airport, and the transfer alone can erase most of what a cheap fare saved.

What you can bring onboard for free

4 rules

Bringing your own food

Allowed on all four airlines — solid snacks and food bought before security are never restricted or charged for.

Liquids and drinks

Standard 100ml/3.4oz security rule applies to anything carried through the checkpoint. Drinks bought airside, after security, have no restriction.

Outside alcohol

Banned on Ryanair and easyJet — you may carry it, but can't drink any alcohol that wasn't served by the cabin crew.

Carrier-specific notes

Ryanair won't let passengers board with a hot drink. easyJet's UK→EU routes can't carry meat or dairy — a customs rule, not an airline policy.
