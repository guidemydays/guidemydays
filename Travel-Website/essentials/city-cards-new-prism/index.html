City Cards

When a city card pays for itself, and when it doesn't.

Compares ten bundled city cards — Paris, Amsterdam, Barcelona, Vienna, Prague, Rome, Bruges, Ljubljana, Copenhagen and Lisboa — on price, what's actually free versus excluded, and how many stops it takes to break even. Some bundle unlimited public transit and some don't, skip-the-line value depends heavily on the season you travel in, and one of the ten (Vienna) is a discount card, not a free-entry one — worth knowing before you buy any of them. Separate from Tours & Tickets, which covers pre-packaged guided tours rather than self-directed passes.

The ten cards

IncludedFree entry to 50+ museums and monuments on their permanent collections — the Louvre, Château de Versailles (Palace & Gardens), Musée d'Orsay, Arc de Triomphe and Sainte-Chapelle among them — plus fast-track entry at most included sites. Temporary exhibitions cost extra even where the venue is covered.
Cost2-day ~€85 ($99) · 4-day ~€105 ($122) · 6-day ~€125 ($145), adult full price. The Louvre still requires its own free timed-entry reservation even with the pass.
Not includedThe Eiffel Tower is not covered at all — always a separate ticket. The pass carries no public transport.
Card pays off whenLouvre + Versailles Palace + Musée d'Orsay already runs close to €85 in separate tickets, so a third flagship site on a 2-day trip is close to a wash and a fourth is a clear win. Skip-the-line matters most April–September; lines are short most winters.

IncludedFree entry to 70+ museums and attractions — the Rijksmuseum, NEMO Science Museum, Stedelijk Museum and ARTIS Zoo among them — plus unlimited GVB transit (tram/bus/metro/ferry), one canal cruise and a day's bike rental.
Cost24h ~€67 ($78) · 48h ~€94 ($109) · 72h ~€115 ($133), adult full price.
Not includedThe Van Gogh Museum (removed from the card in 2022) and the Anne Frank House are not covered — Amsterdam's two most in-demand sights, both needing their own advance timed ticket.
Card pays off whenThe Rijksmuseum plus the included canal cruise already covers most of the 24h price; add NEMO or the day's bike rental and it clears it. Skip-the-line is soft here — it's tap-to-enter, not a formal fast lane, and doesn't reach the two sights with the worst queues.
BuySame price online and at the I amsterdam Store — buying ahead just sets your start time and skips the queue.
Buy at iamsterdam.com ↗
IncludedUnlimited metro/bus/tram/commuter rail, airport included, plus free entry to 25+ museums — Museu Picasso, MNAC and Fundació Joan Miró among them — and assorted dining and shopping discounts.
Cost48h ~€39 ($45) · 72h ~€59 ($68) · 120h ~€79 ($92), adult full price.
Not includedSagrada Família and Park Güell — the city's two most-visited sights — are not free on the base card; both need the pricier all-in upgrade or a separate Gaudí add-on. The standard card gets a discount and fast-track slot at Sagrada Família, not free entry.
Card pays off whenMuseu Picasso + MNAC + Fundació Joan Miró alone equal the 48h price before counting a single metro ride, so three mid-size museums in two days is already break-even.
BuyBuying online at barcelonacard.org runs about 10% cheaper than the same card at a Tourist Info Office in the city — a real price gap, not just a shorter queue.

IncludedUnlimited U-Bahn/tram/bus for the card's duration, plus discounted — never free — admission at 200+ museums, attractions, restaurants and shops, including Schönbrunn Palace and the Belvedere.
Cost24h ~€19 ($22) · 48h ~€31 ($36) · 72h ~€37 ($43), adult full price.
Not includedNothing is free with this card. Unlike every other card here, it's built as transit-plus-discounts rather than free entry — it will surprise anyone expecting the Paris or Amsterdam model.
Card pays off whenA standalone 24h transit ticket alone runs about €6, leaving roughly €13 to claw back — Schönbrunn's discount plus the Belvedere's usually covers most of it, and a third discounted stop is what actually tips it into savings.
BuySame price at wien.info, the Albertinaplatz tourist office and the airport — buying online just avoids the queue.
Buy at wien.info ↗
IncludedUnlimited metro/tram/bus/ferry, including the airport transfer, plus free entry to 70+ sights — the Prague Castle circuit (Circuit B), the Old Town Hall & Astronomical Clock tower, the Jewish Quarter, a Vltava river cruise and a hop-on-hop-off historic tram.
Cost48h 2,700 CZK (~€112 / $130) · 72h 3,300 CZK (~€137 / $159) · 120h 3,900 CZK (~€162 / $188), adult full price.
Not includedEven inside the Castle, the premium Circuit A extras — the Treasury, Powder Tower, the Story of Prague Castle exhibition — and Lobkowicz Palace are separate paid tickets, easy to assume are covered and aren't.
Card pays off whenThe Castle circuit plus the Jewish Quarter alone run to roughly half the 48h price — this pass earns the rest back through the included river cruise, the hop-on-hop-off tram and unlimited transit rather than pure museum-stacking. Skip-the-line matters at the Castle and Petřín Tower in summer, far less November–February.
BuySame price online, via the app, or at Prague City Tourism desks (Old Town Hall, Wenceslas Square, Rytířská) — buying ahead mainly avoids the ticket-office queue.
Buy at praguevisitorpass.eu ↗
IncludedUnlimited bus/tram/metro for the card's duration, free entry to one site (48h) or two (72h) from 100+ participating museums and monuments — the Colosseum/Forum/Palatine combo among them — plus up to 35% off further sites once the free entries are used.
Cost48h ~€38 ($44) · 72h ~€63 ($73), adult full price. The Colosseum still needs its own separate timed-slot booking even with the pass.
Not includedThe Vatican Museums, Sistine Chapel and St Peter's Basilica are not covered — Vatican City is a separate country, the single most common misunderstanding about this pass.
Card pays off whenThe Colosseum/Forum/Palatine combo ticket alone runs close to the 48h price once transit is counted in; a second free entry on the 72h tier plus one discounted stop clears it. Lines run 1–2 hours at the Colosseum June–September, far shorter in winter.
BuySame price on the app, at romapass.it, or at the five Tourist Infopoints — buying ahead mainly locks in an earlier Colosseum time slot, not a lower price.
Buy at romapass.it ↗
IncludedOne free entry each, within 72 hours, to Musea Brugge's roughly dozen sites — the Belfry, Groeningemuseum, Gruuthusemuseum, the Sint-Janshospitaal Memling collection and the Stadhuis among them.
Cost72h only, ~€49 ($57) adult · ~€37 under 26 · ~€25 under 18 — there's no 24h or 48h tier, despite some listings implying otherwise.
Not includedNo public transport — but Bruges' old town is compact and walkable enough that this rarely matters. Each site is a single visit only.
Card pays off whenThe Belfry, Groeningemuseum and Gruuthusemuseum alone run close to the card price; a fourth stop, easy to reach on foot within the 72-hour window, is what clears it.
BuySame price online (museabrugge.be) and at museum ticket desks — buying ahead just skips the queue at your first stop, usually the Belfry.
Buy at museabrugge.be ↗
IncludedUnlimited LPP city buses, the funicular and admission up to Ljubljana Castle, a guided Old Town walking tour, a Ljubljanica river boat cruise, and free entry to 30+ sites — plus, unusually for a card this size, a cable car ride to Velika Planina outside the city.
Cost24h ~€41 ($48) · 48h ~€49 ($57) · 72h ~€54 ($63), adult full price.
Not includedPlečnik House, a major architecture sight, still needs its own advance booking even with the card. Every included site is a single visit.
Card pays off whenThe Castle-and-funicular ticket plus the river cruise alone run close to the 24h price before a single bus ride is counted — the included walking tour and the Velika Planina cable car push it clearly into savings from there. Queues here are rarely long even in summer; this is a bundling card, not a time-saving one.
BuyThe official site advertises a 10% online discount that doesn't always apply at checkout — budget for the listed price either way. Buying online mainly saves a trip to a Tourist Information Centre.
Buy at visitljubljana.com ↗
IncludedFree entry to 80+ Capital Region attractions — Rosenborg Castle, Christiansborg Palace, the National Museum of Denmark and the Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek among them — plus unlimited transit including the airport metro. Up to two children under 12 travel and enter free with each adult card.
Cost24h ~589 DKK (~€79 / $92) · 48h ~859 DKK (~€115 / $134) · 72h ~1,039 DKK (~€139 / $162), adult, Discover tier. A cheaper city-only Hop tier also exists — check the current price before assuming it's actually less.
Not includedTivoli Gardens is garden admission only — rides are extra even with the card. Rosenborg means swapping the card for a timed ticket at the counter rather than walking straight in, and Denmark's national museums are no longer free general admission on their own.
Card pays off whenRosenborg, Christiansborg's Royal Reception Rooms and the National Museum together already exceed the 24h card price before counting transit — a fourth stop or Tivoli's garden entry is a clear win from there. Lines build at Tivoli and Rosenborg June–August; short the rest of the year.
BuyOnline or in the app only — there's no walk-up purchase any more. The card activates the moment you redeem it, so buy close to when you'll actually start using it.
Buy at copenhagencard.com ↗
IncludedUnlimited metro, Carris buses and trams (Tram 28 included), funiculars, the Santa Justa Lift, CP trains to Sintra and Cascais, and metro to/from the airport — plus free entry to 50+ museums and monuments, including Belém Tower, Jerónimos Monastery, the National Coach Museum and São Jorge Castle.
Cost24h ~€31 ($36) · 48h ~€51 ($59) · 72h ~€62 ($72), adult full price. Prices are set through March 2027.
Not includedThe "fast-track" at busy venues is a pre-booked timed slot, not a literal skip-the-line — you still queue with everyone else holding that slot.
Card pays off whenBelém Tower plus Jerónimos Monastery alone are close to the 24h price before counting a single metro ride; add the Sintra round-trip train and it's a clear win. Belém and Jerónimos queues commonly run 2+ hours on summer weekends — the timed slot genuinely helps then, far less on a quiet weekday.
BuyBuying online at shop.visitlisboa.com carries a real discount — around 5% off the 72h card at last check — on top of skipping the ticket-office line at Praça do Comércio, the airport or Belém.
Buy at lisboacard.org ↗
