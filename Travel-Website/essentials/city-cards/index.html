City Cards

When a city card pays for itself, and when it doesn't.

Compares ten bundled city cards — Paris, Amsterdam, Barcelona, Vienna, Prague, Rome, Bruges, Ljubljana, Copenhagen and Lisboa — on price, what's actually free versus excluded, and how many stops it takes to break even. Some bundle unlimited public transit and some don't, skip-the-line value depends heavily on the season you travel in, and one of the ten (Vienna) is a discount card, not a free-entry one — worth knowing before you buy any of them. Separate from Tours & Tickets, which covers pre-packaged guided tours rather than self-directed passes.
