Airport Connections

The minimum airlines sell you, and what to give yourself.

Minimum connection times at 45 major hub airports, split by connection type, with the layout and immigration quirks that make each one faster or slower than the number suggests. The minimums are what airlines will sell you — the "book at least" column is what to give yourself. Every figure is approximate: published minimums differ by airline, terminal pairing, and season, so treat this as a planning guide and confirm the exact minimum with your carrier.

All figures assume one ticket with bags checked through. On separate tickets you re-check bags and clear security again — allow three hours anywhere, four at the red hubs.
The slow step is almost never walking — it is passport control. Any connection that crosses an immigration border (Schengen boundary, US or Canadian entry, terminal-change re-entry) needs the higher number.
If you miss a connection on one ticket the airline rebooks you free. Miss one across two tickets and the second flight is simply gone — that gap is the whole reason to overbuy time.
Last flight of the day, one bag, tight budget: buy the longer layover. The cheapest itinerary is usually the one with the shortest connection for a reason.
