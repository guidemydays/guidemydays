Cultural Faux Pas & Taboos

Fine at home, not fine everywhere.

A country-by-country reference for the cultural missteps travelers make most often — not crime risk (that’s the Safety Guide) and not logistics like visas or plugs (that’s Before You Go), but the everyday behavior that marks a visitor as one who didn’t do the reading. Each entry names what not to do, why it matters to the people who live there, and what to do instead. Filter by region, or search a country.
