Entry Requirements

The pre-entry authorizations and eVisas a traveler has to arrange before boarding — ESTA-style travel authorizations, electronic visas and online arrival cards — with what each costs and how far ahead to apply. Eligibility for each system varies by passport, so confirm yours on the official site linked below. Countries that need a full visa application are on the Visas page, which answers for your own passport specifically. Rules here change with little notice, so the page also tracks the ones currently in flux, including ETIAS.

ETIAS has been delayed. In July 2026, EU-Lisa confirmed that a Q4 2026 launch was no longer feasible after technical issues with the EES border system. A revised timeline is expected in late 2026. Visa-exempt visitors will eventually need a quick online pre-authorization (€20, valid 3 years) to visit France, Italy, Spain, Germany, and 27 other Schengen countries — similar in shape to the US's own ESTA. No action needed until a launch date is confirmed.

Everywhere Else

Which countries are visa-free with no pre-travel step at all depends entirely on which passport you're holding — a list that is visa-free for one nationality can require a full visa for another. The Visas page answers this properly: pick your own passport there and see every destination in the matrix grouped by exactly what it needs, not a fixed example list. Standard rules still apply wherever no visa is needed: a passport valid at least 6 months beyond arrival, and no overstaying.

Entry rules change. Verify requirements with the destination's own immigration authority before every trip — especially for ETIAS, which has been delayed past 2026 with no confirmed new launch date.
