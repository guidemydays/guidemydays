Grocery & Pharmacy Delivery Abroad

The supermarket on the corner has a different name in every country.

Supermarket and pharmacy chain names by country, so you recognize one on sight instead of hunting for an unfamiliar logo — plus which grocery and pharmacy delivery services actually operate there, and whether they deliver to a hotel or a short-term rental. No prices and no what-to-buy advice: this is a where-to-buy and delivery-availability reference only. For what to pack from home before you go, see Travel Packing; for water safety, see Tap Water.

30 countries across five regions. Chains are the ones a visitor is most likely to actually see on the street — this is not a ranking or a recommendation of one over another.

Finding the nearest one

Search the chain's name, or just "supermarket" / "pharmacy", on Google Maps or Apple Maps once you have your hotel or Airbnb's exact address — every GuideMyDays guide's hotel banner lists it. Both show current opening hours and whether a branch is open right now.

In city centers, a small-format branch of the country's dominant chain (Tesco Express, Carrefour City, Sainsbury's Local, a 7-Eleven) is usually within a few minutes' walk of any central hotel, even where the full-size supermarket is further out.

Hotel vs. Airbnb: how delivery arrives

Hotel — most hotels don't let a courier up to the room directly. Give the delivery app the hotel's name and street address, and add "hold for guest [name], arriving via front desk" in the delivery notes. The front desk holds the order and calls or texts the room; ask at check-in if you're expecting a delivery so staff know to expect it.

Short-term rental — there's no front desk, so building access matters more than the street address: unit number, buzzer or intercom code, gate or lockbox code, and floor. Put that in the app's delivery notes, and message your host in advance if the building needs a code or a doorman to let a courier in. Without an answer, most couriers leave the order at the main entrance or mailbox area.

Europe
France Europe
Supermarkets
Carrefour, Monoprix, Franprix, Casino.
Pharmacies
No single national chain — every pharmacy carries the same green cross sign; the Pharmacie de Garde system designates which one stays open nights, Sundays and holidays.
Delivery
Carrefour Livraison, Monoprix+ (via Amazon); Uber Eats and Deliveroo both carry a "Courses" (groceries) tab in major cities.
Italy Europe
Supermarkets
Conad, Esselunga, Coop, Carrefour.
Pharmacies
Farmacia — independent, marked by a green cross; no single dominant chain, though multi-city networks like LloydsFarmacia also operate.
Delivery
Esselunga a Casa, Carrefour Italia; Glovo and Just Eat both carry grocery sections in larger cities.
Spain Europe
Supermarkets
Mercadona, Carrefour, Dia.
Pharmacies
Farmacia — independent, green cross sign; Spanish law restricts pharmacy ownership to licensed pharmacists, so there's no nationwide chain.
Delivery
Mercadona's own online ordering, Carrefour Online; Glovo carries both grocery and pharmacy delivery in most cities.
Portugal Europe
Supermarkets
Continente, Pingo Doce, Lidl.
Pharmacies
Farmácia — independent, green cross sign; no single dominant national chain.
Delivery
Continente Online; Uber Eats and Glovo both carry grocery sections.
United Kingdom Europe
Supermarkets
Tesco, Sainsbury's, Waitrose, ASDA.
Pharmacies
Boots, Superdrug.
Delivery
Ocado, Tesco and Sainsbury's each run their own delivery; Deliveroo and Uber Eats both carry grocery-shop sections for smaller top-up orders.
Germany Europe
Supermarkets
Rewe, Edeka, Lidl, Aldi.
Pharmacies
dm and Rossmann are drugstores for toiletries and OTC items; prescription medicine only comes from an Apotheke, independently owned and marked with a red "A" sign.
Delivery
Rewe Lieferservice; quick-commerce apps (Flink and similar) have consolidated repeatedly in Germany — confirm one is still live in-app before relying on it.
Switzerland Europe
Supermarkets
Migros, Coop.
Pharmacies
Amavita, Sun Store — both national chains.
Delivery
Coop@home, LeShop.ch (Migros-owned).
Netherlands Europe
Supermarkets
Albert Heijn (AH), Jumbo.
Pharmacies
Kruidvat and Etos are drugstores for OTC items and toiletries; prescription medicine comes from an Apotheek.
Delivery
AH's own bezorgservice, Picnic (grocery-only quick delivery); the earlier wave of 10-minute delivery apps has shrunk sharply, so check current app-store availability.
Greece Europe
Supermarkets
AB Vasilopoulos, Sklavenitis, Carrefour.
Pharmacies
Farmakeio — independent, green cross sign; no single dominant chain.
Delivery
e-fresh (AB Vasilopoulos), Sklavenitis online; Wolt is very widely used for both restaurant and grocery delivery.
North America
United States North America
Supermarkets
Walmart, Kroger, Safeway, Publix, Whole Foods — exact chain coverage varies by state and city.
Pharmacies
CVS, Walgreens, Rite Aid.
Delivery
Instacart (multi-retailer), Amazon Fresh / Whole Foods via Amazon, Walmart+; CVS and Walgreens each run their own same-day delivery for prescriptions and OTC items.
Canada North America
Supermarkets
Loblaws, Sobeys, Metro.
Pharmacies
Shoppers Drug Mart (Pharmaprix in Quebec), Rexall.
Delivery
Instacart operates nationwide across most major chains; Shoppers Drug Mart runs its own same-day delivery in larger cities.
Mexico North America
Supermarkets
Walmart de México (Bodega Aurrera, Walmart Express), Soriana, Chedraui.
Pharmacies
Farmacias Guadalajara, Farmacias del Ahorro, Farmacias Similares — the last also runs low-cost walk-in clinics.
Delivery
Rappi is dominant for both groceries and pharmacy items; Uber Eats carries a grocery tab in major cities.

Asia-Pacific
Japan Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
AEON, Ito-Yokado, Life; convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) work as everyday grocery top-up stops.
Pharmacies
Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Sundrug, Sugi Pharmacy — drugstores for toiletries and OTC items; prescriptions are filled at a separate yakkyoku (薬局).
Delivery
Amazon Fresh (Tokyo and Osaka areas only), Rakuten Seiyu Netsuper — coverage is far more limited outside major metro areas than in the US or Europe.
China Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
RT-Mart, Carrefour China, Walmart China; Sam's Club is also present in major cities.
Pharmacies
Independent pharmacies (药店) are everywhere and unbranded nationally, though chains like Yifeng Pharmacy operate regionally.
Delivery
Meituan and Ele.me both dominate — the same apps used for restaurant delivery also carry full grocery and pharmacy sections, and are the default way most residents shop.
South Korea Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
E-Mart, Lotte Mart, Homeplus.
Pharmacies
Independent pharmacies (약국), marked by a cross sign — no dominant nationwide retail chain; prescriptions and OTC medicine are both handled there.
Delivery
Coupang is the dominant e-commerce/grocery platform, known for next-day and even same-day "Rocket" delivery; Baemin also carries grocery sections.
Singapore Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
FairPrice, Cold Storage, Sheng Siong.
Pharmacies
Guardian, Watsons — drugstore chains for OTC items and toiletries; prescriptions are filled at a polyclinic, hospital, or select outlets with a licensed pharmacist.
Delivery
FairPrice Group's own app; Grab's GrabMart carries groceries and pharmacy items across the city.
Thailand Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
Tops Market, Big C, Villa Market — Villa Market is popular with expats and tourists for imported goods.
Pharmacies
Boots and Watsons both operate in Thailand; independent pharmacies are common and can dispense many medicines over the counter that need a prescription elsewhere.
Delivery
Grab's GrabMart and LINE MAN both carry full grocery and pharmacy sections and are the standard way to order either in Bangkok and other major cities.
Vietnam Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
Co.opmart, WinMart, GO! (formerly Big C).
Pharmacies
Pharmacity, Long Châu — both national chains, easy to spot by signage.
Delivery
Grab's GrabMart is the standard app for both groceries and pharmacy items in major cities.
Philippines Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
SM Supermarket, Robinsons Supermarket, Puregold.
Pharmacies
Mercury Drug — by far the largest and most recognizable chain, found in nearly every neighborhood and mall; Watsons.
Delivery
GrabMart and foodpanda both carry grocery and pharmacy sections; Mercury Drug also runs its own delivery in Metro Manila and other major cities.
Indonesia Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
Indomaret and Alfamart function as the everyday top-up stop on nearly every block; Hypermart and Transmart cover full shops.
Pharmacies
Kimia Farma, Guardian, Watsons.
Delivery
GrabMart and Gojek's GoFood/GoMart both dominate — the same super-apps used for ride-hailing also carry grocery and pharmacy delivery.
India Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
Reliance Fresh, Reliance Smart, DMart, Spencer's — exact chain presence varies significantly by city.
Pharmacies
Apollo Pharmacy, MedPlus — both national chains with a large footprint in major cities.
Delivery
Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy's Instamart dominate quick grocery delivery in major metros, often under 15 minutes; 1mg and Apollo 24|7 handle pharmacy delivery, frequently same-day.
Australia Asia-Pacific
Supermarkets
Coles, Woolworths, IGA.
Pharmacies
Chemist Warehouse — the largest chain by far and known for discount pricing; Priceline Pharmacy, Terry White Chemmart.
Delivery
Coles Online and Woolworths each run their own delivery; Amazon Fresh in Sydney and Melbourne; Uber Eats carries a grocery/pharmacy tab in most cities.
New Zealand Asia-Pacific

Pharmacies
Unichem, Life Pharmacy — both national franchise networks.
