Island Hopping

More than one island, planned so the transport doesn't ruin the trip.

Multi-island itinerary circuits for five archipelagos — the recommended island sequence, ferry vs. flight legs, minimum time per stop, best season, and the transport mistake that costs most travelers a day. Covers the Greek Cyclades, Thailand's Gulf islands into Cambodia, the Philippines, the Azores, and the Canary Islands. For individual island picks rather than a multi-stop route, see Best Islands.

How to plan an island-hopping circuit

Ferry vs. flight isn't just about speed
Ferries are usually cheaper and let you watch the crossing. Flights are the only option once islands aren't on the same ferry network, and worth it past about three hours by boat.
Give every stop at least two nights
A one-night island stop spends half its time on transfers. Stacking three or more one-night stays is the itinerary that never lands anywhere long enough to enjoy it.
Season shapes the transport, not just the weather
Inter-island ferry frequency — and cancellation risk from wind or swell — swings by season far more than the islands' own climate does. Check the schedule, not just the forecast.
Never book an onward flight for the same day
A delayed ferry or small regional flight on the last inter-island leg is common. One buffer night before any onward or return flight is the cheapest insurance on the trip.
Europe
Greek CycladesMykonos → Naxos → Paros → Santorini
FERRY
Fly into Athens, ferry from Piraeus to Mykonos, then hop island-to-island toward Santorini — Naxos and Paros sit on the same ferry corridor and add almost no backtracking.
Ferry vs. flightHigh-speed ferries (Blue Star Ferries, SeaJets) run direct between every stop on this route; flying only makes sense for the one-off Athens↔Santorini leg on a tight schedule — there's no flight between the smaller islands themselves.
Minimum time per stop2 nights on Mykonos, Naxos and Paros; 3 on Santorini.
Best seasonLate May–June or September–early October — full ferry timetables without July–August's heat, crowds, or fare spikes.
Mistake to avoidBooking a flight home for the same day as the last ferry. The meltemi wind grounds high-speed catamarans fleet-wide with a day's notice or less; only the slower conventional ferries keep running.
How to bookFerryhopper.com compares every operator on one route; book July–August sailings 4–6 weeks out.
Southeast Asia
Thailand's Gulf Islands into CambodiaKoh Samui → Koh Phangan → Koh Tao → Koh Rong
FERRY + FLIGHT
Fly Bangkok–Koh Samui, catamaran to Koh Phangan then Koh Tao, then a combined bus-and-boat ticket across the land border to Koh Rong or Sihanoukville.
Ferry vs. flightBangkok Airways covers Bangkok–Samui in under an hour (the overland-plus-ferry alternative runs 10+ hours); Lomprayah and Seatran catamarans link Samui, Phangan and Tao in 40 minutes to 1.5 hours each.
Minimum time per stop2 nights each on Samui and Phangan; 3 on Tao if diving.
Best seasonDecember–March, the Gulf of Thailand's dry season with calm seas; ferry frequency drops and some routes suspend outright during the October–November monsoon.
Mistake to avoidBuying the Thailand-to-Cambodia "through ticket" as if it were one boat. It's several separate transfers by different operators with a land-border wait in between — a delay on the first leg isn't held for you on the next.
How to book12Go Asia for the multi-leg Cambodia crossing; Lomprayah or Seatran direct for the inter-island catamarans.
Philippines — Palawan + VisayasEl Nido → Coron → Cebu → Bohol
FERRY + FLIGHT
Fly Manila–El Nido, ferry down to Coron, fly Coron/Manila–Cebu (no sea route covers this stretch), then a fast ferry on to Bohol or Siquijor.
Ferry vs. flightEl Nido–Coron runs by fastcraft along Palawan's coast in about 4 hours; Coron to the Visayas has no practical ferry at all — it's a domestic flight on Cebu Pacific or PAL Express, usually routed through Manila.
Minimum time per stop3 nights El Nido, 2 Coron, 2–3 Bohol or Siquijor.
Best seasonDecember–May, before typhoon season. July–October sees Palawan's outrigger and fastcraft routes cancelled with little notice.
Mistake to avoidBooking a flight out for the same day as an inter-island ferry. Palawan fastcrafts cancel outright on rough seas even in nominal dry-season weeks, with no guaranteed rebooking.
How to book2Go Travel or OceanJet direct for the ferries; Cebu Pacific or PAL Express direct for the Palawan–Visayas flight, since nothing else connects them.
Atlantic
AzoresSão Miguel → Terceira → Faial → Pico
FLIGHT
Fly Ponta Delgada (São Miguel) to Terceira, then on to Faial, with a short ferry across to Pico.
Ferry vs. flightSATA Azores Airlines runs the only scheduled inter-island network and covers every leg here except the last; the one reliable ferry is Faial↔Pico (Atlantico Line, about 30 minutes, several times daily year-round) — pair those two islands rather than trying to fly between them.
Minimum time per stop2 nights each on São Miguel and Terceira; 2 combined for Faial and Pico.
Best seasonJune–September, when SATA's inter-island frequency is highest; winter sees fewer flights and more weather delays from Atlantic storm systems.
Mistake to avoidBooking SATA inter-island legs as a separate ticket from the transatlantic flight in. A missed connection on a delayed inter-island hop isn't protected, and the next flight to the same island may not run until the next day.
How to bookSATA Azores Airlines direct — book each inter-island hop 2+ months ahead in summer, the planes are small and seats sell out. Atlantico Line's Faial–Pico ferry needs no advance booking most of the year.
Canary IslandsTenerife → Gran Canaria → Lanzarote
FERRY + FLIGHT
Ferry or fly Tenerife to Gran Canaria, then on to Lanzarote or Fuerteventura — every pair on this route is covered both ways.
Ferry vs. flightFred Olsen Express and Naviera Armas ferries connect every island (Tenerife–Gran Canaria about 1.5 hours, Lanzarote–Fuerteventura about 25 minutes) and let you bring a rental car; Binter Canarias and Canaryfly fly the same routes in 30–40 minutes and are faster for the longer Gran Canaria–Lanzarote hop.
Minimum time per stop3 nights on Tenerife or Gran Canaria; 2 on Lanzarote or Fuerteventura.
Best seasonYear-round — the appeal is the mild climate — but ferries run fullest and cheapest outside Christmas/New Year and Easter week.
Mistake to avoidBoarding a ferry with a rental car without pre-booking the vehicle space. Passenger tickets are usually available same-day; vehicle spaces on the popular routes sell out days ahead in summer.
How to bookDirectferries.com or Fred Olsen Express/Naviera Armas direct for ferries; Binter Canarias direct for flights — often cheaper booked ahead than a ferry-plus-car combo.
