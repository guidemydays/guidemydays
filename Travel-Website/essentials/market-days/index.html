Market Days

Where the locals shop, and what day to show up.

Sixteen markets across thirteen cities that have run on a fixed weekly rhythm for years, not a one-off event — day, hours, and what's actually on sale, from Borough Market's cheese counters to Istanbul's Grand Bazaar. Every entry links to its city guide. This isn't every market on earth, just the ones stable and well-known enough to plan a morning around.

BangkokThailand
Chatuchak Weekend MarketBazaar
Saturday–Sunday · roughly 9:00–18:00
Chatuchak district
One of the world's largest weekend markets — more than 8,000 stalls across 27 sections, selling everything from antiques to streetwear to houseplants.
BarcelonaSpain
La BoqueriaFood & produce
Monday–Saturday · 8:00–20:30 · closed Sunday
La Rambla, Ciutat Vella
Barcelona's flagship food market, just off La Rambla — produce, seafood, jamón, and tapas counters under a wrought-iron and stained-glass roof.
BerlinGermany
Mauerpark FlohmarktFlea & antiques
Sundays only · roughly 9:00–18:00
Prenzlauer Berg
Berlin's best-known flea market, spread across the park that once sat inside the Wall's death strip — secondhand clothes, records, and furniture.
IstanbulTurkey
Grand Bazaar (Kapalıçarşı)Bazaar
Monday–Saturday · 9:00–19:00 · closed Sunday
Fatih, between Beyazıt and Nuruosmaniye
One of the world's oldest and largest covered markets — more than 4,000 shops under vaulted stone ceilings, trading on this site since the 15th century.
Spice Bazaar (Mısır Çarşısı)Food & produce
Daily · roughly 8:00–19:30, shorter Sunday
Eminönü, by the Golden Horn
A smaller, L-shaped covered market — spices, Turkish delight, and dried fruit stalls that have traded here since the 1660s.
KyotoJapan
Nishiki MarketFood & produce
Daily · most shops roughly 9:00–18:00
Nakagyo-ku
A five-block covered arcade known as "Kyoto's Kitchen" — pickles, knives, tea, and street food from shops that have traded here for generations. Some close Wednesdays.
LondonEngland
Borough MarketFood & produce
Monday–Saturday · 10:00–17:00, fullest Wed–Sat · closed Sunday
Southwark, by London Bridge station
London's oldest food market, trading on this site since the 13th century — cheesemongers, fishmongers, and prepared-food stalls fanning out from London Bridge.
Portobello Road MarketFlea & antiques
Antiques Saturday, 8:00–18:00 · general market Mon–Sat
Notting Hill
Nearly a mile of stalls along one street — antiques and vintage dominate Saturdays, with fruit, veg, and fashion trading the rest of the week.
Camden MarketCrafts & design
Daily · most stalls roughly 10:00–18:00
Camden Town
A cluster of connected markets along Regent's Canal — crafts, vintage clothing, and street food packed into old stables and warehouses.
MadridSpain
Mercado de San MiguelFood & produce
Daily · roughly 10:00–24:00, later Thu–Sat
La Latina, next to Plaza Mayor
A restored 1916 iron-and-glass market building turned upscale food hall — tapas, wine, and pintxos counters rather than raw produce stalls.
ParisFrance
Marché d'AligreFood & produce
Tuesday–Sunday mornings · roughly 7:30–13:30 · closed Monday
Place d'Aligre, 12th arrondissement
One of Paris's last true neighborhood markets — a covered food hall and open-air produce stalls, with a small flea market for secondhand goods in the same square.
SantiagoChile
Mercado CentralFood & produce
Daily · roughly 6:00–17:00, shorter Sunday
Centro, near Estación Mapocho
A wrought-iron market hall built in 1872 — fish and seafood stalls surround the sit-down marisquerías at its center, a Santiago lunch institution.
São PauloBrazil
Mercado Municipal (Mercadão)Food & produce
Daily · roughly 6:00–18:00, Sunday until 16:00
Centro
A 1933 stained-glass market hall famous for its mortadella sandwiches — produce, spices, and dried-fruit stalls beneath one of the city's grandest interiors.
TokyoJapan
Tsukiji Outer MarketFood & produce
Roughly 5:00–14:00 · many closed Sunday, hours vary by shop
Tsukiji, Chuo
The retail market that stayed when Tsukiji's wholesale fish auction moved to Toyosu — sushi counters, knife shops, and grilled seafood skewers in narrow lanes.
VeniceItaly
Rialto Market (Mercato di Rialto)Food & produce
Monday–Saturday mornings · roughly 7:00–13:30
San Polo, foot of the Rialto Bridge
Venice's produce and fish market, trading beside the Grand Canal for centuries — the fish hall (Pescaria) is closed Sunday and Monday.
ViennaAustria
NaschmarktFood & produce
Monday–Saturday · roughly 6:00–19:30, Sat until 18:00 · closed Sunday
Between Wieden and Mariahilf
Vienna's largest and oldest market — produce, spices, and food stalls running along a narrow strip; a flea market takes over the western end on Saturdays.
