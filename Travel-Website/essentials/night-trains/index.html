Berth types — pick before you book Most trains offer at least two of these
Seat Budget
Reclining chair. Fine for journeys under 6 hours; uncomfortable for longer runs. Cheapest option by far.
Couchette Mid-range
Fold-down bunk in a shared 4- or 6-berth compartment. Bedding supplied on most European services. Best value-to-rest ratio.
Sleeper cabin Premium
Private or semi-private locked cabin, 1–3 berths. Breakfast included on many European services. Sells out months in advance.
Deluxe / en-suite Luxury
Private cabin with shower and toilet. Available on Nightjet Vienna–Amsterdam, the Caledonian Sleeper, and select Asian services.

Japan's only regular overnight train left, and it books out the moment the reservation window opens. The carpeted Nobinobi berth is covered by a rail pass and is the cheapest bed in the country — a flat mat, not a bunk.

What to bring on a night train The essentials most people forget
Eye mask + earplugs
Couchette compartments have uneven lighting and door noise through the night. These two items separate a rested arrival from a groggy one.
Small padlock
Couchette luggage racks have no lock built in. Loop your bag strap through the rail and secure it — most seasoned night-train riders carry a small padlock for exactly this.
Water bottle
Compartments get warm overnight and the dining car closes before midnight. A full bottle keeps you comfortable without a middle-of-the-night walk down the corridor.
Power bank
Couchette compartments rarely have sockets. Sleeper cabins usually do, but the position is often awkward to reach from the bunk. Charge everything the night before and bring a bank as backup.
Printed reservation
Conductors often board between 23:00 and 02:00 to check tickets. Patchy signal makes a downloaded copy safer than hoping an app loads — keep it within reach of your bunk.
Toiletry kit in hand luggage
Your main bag goes on the overhead rack or under the bottom bunk. Keep a face wash, toothbrush, and a change of top layer in a small bag at your seat — a morning routine without digging through the whole pack.
