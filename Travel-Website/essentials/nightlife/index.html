International Nightlife Guide

Same night out, nine completely different rulebooks.

Bar and club hours, dress codes, door culture, and the safest way home after last call — city by city, for the fleet’s nine biggest nightlife destinations: Madrid, Barcelona, Berlin, Ibiza, Bangkok, Tokyo, New York City, London, and Lisbon. Norms shift between neighborhoods and specific venues; treat each entry as the general pattern for that city, not a guarantee at every door.

How to read the arc: the strip under each city runs 6pm to 8am in that city’s own time. The tinted band is when bars fill, the bright band is when rooms peak, the dark mark is when late transport stops, and the pulsing dot is where that city is right now.
