What each site is good for, and where it falls short.

The travel guides, booking tools and reference sites worth your time, each with a straight assessment of what it is good for and where it falls short. It says plainly which ones have stopped publishing and are now archives rather than current sources. Meant as a starting point for the research this site does not cover itself.
