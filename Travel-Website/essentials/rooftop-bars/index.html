Rooftop Bars & Skyline Views

The skyline is the same for everyone. Getting a seat in front of it never is.

Reservation policy, dress code, minimum spend, and how to time your arrival before sunset — for 19 rooftop bars across 10 cities: Bangkok, Tokyo, Singapore, Hong Kong, Paris, London, Dubai, New York, Chicago and Los Angeles. Every detail below is sourced from the venue's own reservation page or its hotel's site where one exists; dress codes and minimum spends shift by season and night of the week, so confirm on the operator's own site before a special-occasion visit. Asia-Pacific
Bangkok, Thailand Moon Bar (Vertigo & Moon Bar, Banyan Tree Bangkok) 61st floor, on the hotel's converted helipad — a 360° view over the city Currently Closed for renovation from mid-June 2026, with an expected reopening around 28 November 2026 (subject to change) — confirm on Banyan Tree's own page before planning around it. Access & dress Smart casual — no shorts or flip-flops; closed shoes and long pants expected of gentlemen. Reservation Moon Bar (the bar side) has historically been walk-in, no booking needed; Vertigo (the restaurant side) takes reservations. In normal operation the bar opens 5pm, restaurant 6–10:30pm. Cost No cover for the bar; à la carte cocktails and a full dinner menu on the restaurant side. Walk-in OK
Bangkok, Thailand Above Eleven 33rd–34th floor, Fraser Suites Sukhumvit Access & dress Smart casual — shorts allowed for both women and men, but sandals, flip-flops and Birkenstocks are refused; men need fully covered shoes and no sleeveless tops. Reservation Recommended, especially for dinner — book online via Above Eleven's own site for an instant confirmation. Open 5pm–2am. Cost No stated cover; a Peruvian-Japanese à la carte and cocktail menu. Reserve ahead
Tokyo, Japan Rooftop Bar, Andaz Tokyo Toranomon Hills 52nd floor, a semi-open-air roof-protected terrace over the Tokyo skyline Access & dress Smart casual; no stated hard dress code beyond that. Reservation Accepted by phone, WhatsApp or email via Andaz Tokyo's own booking page, up to two months ahead — bookable arrivals are only taken up to 8pm, so a later visit is walk-in. Cost No seating charge for hotel guests, or for anyone 5–8pm; a ¥2,200 per-person seating charge applies on the terrace after 8pm (March–December). Reserve ahead
Singapore 1-Altitude 63rd floor, Raffles Place — the tallest building in the district Access & dress Smart casual — no singlets, sleeveless tops, shorts or flip-flops for men. Minimum entry age 21; after 10pm the floor is men 25+ / women 21+. Reservation Recommended for a group or a table for sunset; the bar itself takes walk-ins. Cost No stated cover for standing room; table minimums apply on request. Reserve ahead
Singapore CÉ LA VI Singapore — SkyBar Level 57, Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Access & dress Dressy/smart casual — no slippers, sandals, beachwear or activewear; men need covered shoes (no crocs) and a sleeved shirt; smart shorts allowed for men before 10pm. Reservation Required — book via Marina Bay Sands' own page. Sold as timed sessions (Sunset 4–7:30pm, Late Night from 8pm) rather than a walk-up table. Cost Minimum spend from S$200++ per person on the Sunset Session, from S$1,000++ per table Late Night — figures move with the calendar and public holidays. Reservation required
Hong Kong Ozone, The Ritz-Carlton Hong Kong 118th floor of the ICC tower — the world's highest bar Access & dress No flip-flops or athletic wear; men should avoid open-toe shoes, sleeveless shirts and sports shorts. Reservation Recommended — book via Ozone's own site or by phone. Cost No stated cover; cocktails and small plates à la carte. Reserve ahead
Hong Kong Aqua Spirit 17th floor, H Zentre, Tsim Sha Tsui — over Victoria Harbour Access & dress Smart casual — no shorts, open-toe shoes or sleeveless shirts for gentlemen at dinner. Under-18s are not admitted to the bar from 6pm onward. Reservation Not needed for drinks — walk in; reservations are for the adjoining Italian/Japanese dining rooms via Aqua's own page. Cost No cover; cocktails à la carte. Walk-in OK
Europe
Paris, France Le Perchoir Ménilmontant The original Le Perchoir rooftop, on top of a former industrial building near Ménilmontant métro Access & dress Smart casual. Reservation Walk-in for the rooftop bar itself — arrive early to skip the queue, or book a table downstairs at the 6th-floor restaurant instead. Cost No cover; cocktails, wine by the glass or bottle, and bar snacks from a snack shack. Walk-in OK
Paris, France Terrass″ Rooftop Bar Atop the Terrass″ Hôtel on the Montmartre hill — one of the highest rooftop views in Paris Access & dress Smart casual; no stated hard dress code. Reservation Walk-in, no booking needed for the bar — the hotel's own page suggests avoiding 7–8:30pm to skip the wait. Cost No cover; cocktails à la carte. Walk-in OK
London, United Kingdom Aqua Shard Level 31, The Shard Access & dress Smart casual — tailored shorts allowed until 5pm; no sportswear or flip-flops at any time. Reservation Opens on a rolling three-month window via Aqua Shard's own site; every table has a skyline view, so window seats can't be specifically requested. Cost £50 minimum spend per guest on a bar-only booking (dining bookings are separate). Reserve ahead
London, United Kingdom SUSHISAMBA London 38th–39th floor, Heron Tower Access & dress Smart casual — smart trainers are fine; tracksuits, denim shorts, flip-flops/sliders and beachwear are refused, entry at the door's discretion. Reservation Recommended via SUSHISAMBA's own site. Cost No stated cover; à la carte Japanese-Brazilian-Peruvian menu and cocktails. Reserve ahead

Reservation Required — call or email via At.mosphere's own contact page. Cost No general cover; a $15 per-person cover applies after 8pm Friday and Saturday. Walk-in OK

Chicago, United States Cindy's Rooftop 13th floor, Chicago Athletic Association, under a glass atrium Access & dress No strict dress code; smart casual encouraged. Reservation Not required — encouraged for indoor seating via Cindy's own site. Cost No stated cover; à la carte. Walk-in OK
