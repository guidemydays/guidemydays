Every city, placed on the advisory scale.

Every city and island with a guide on this site, placed in the four US State Department advisory levels — normal precautions, increased caution, reconsider travel, do not travel — with the current standing notices called out above the list. Advisory levels move with events and can be raised or lowered without warning; check the live advisory on travel.state.gov before you book or fly.

UAE, Qatar, Oman & Jordan — Level 3 Since 2026

The State Dept issued Level 3 "Reconsider Travel" advisories for the UAE, Qatar, Oman, and Jordan citing regional armed-conflict risks — including the threat of drone and missile attacks following US–Iran hostilities — and authorized non-emergency US government personnel to depart the UAE and Qatar. Monitor each country's advisory closely before any travel to Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Doha, Muscat, or Petra.
