Great Self-Drive Routes

Fourteen roads worth renting a car just to drive.

The self-drive routes worth planning a trip around, with the total distance, the stops along the way, how demanding the road actually is, the best season to go, and what kind of car it takes. Car Rental Around the World covers booking the vehicle — this page is the route itself. Filter by region to see what's reachable from where you're already headed.

Europe
Ring Road
Route 1 · Iceland
↔
Distance About 1,320 km (820 mi) — the full loop
Country
Iceland
Key Stops Golden Circle detour, Vík í Mýrdal, Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon & Diamond Beach, Skaftafell, Mývatn, Akureyri, Seyðisfjörður
Road Difficulty Fully paved and mostly two-lane, but single-lane bridges and blind rises are common. Side trips onto F-road highland tracks need a 4x4 and are only open in summer; ice, wind and short daylight make winter driving demanding.
Best Season June–August for reliable roads and long daylight; May and September are quieter with a real chance of snow at higher points
Recommended Car A standard 2WD covers the ring road itself in summer; a 4x4 is worth it for any F-road detour or a winter trip

Amalfi Coast Drive
SS163 · Italy
↔
Distance About 50 km (30 mi) between Sorrento/Positano and Vietri sul Mare/Salerno
Country
Italy
Key Stops Positano, Praiano, Amalfi, Ravello, Minori, Vietri sul Mare
Road Difficulty Extremely narrow with continuous hairpin turns along a cliff edge, plus heavy tour-bus traffic in summer — routinely rated one of the more stressful drives in Europe
Best Season May–June or September–October; many travelers skip driving it in August and take the ferry or bus instead
Recommended Car The smallest car you can rent — width matters more than power on this road

North Coast 500
Scotland, United Kingdom
↔
Distance 516 miles (830 km) — a loop starting and ending at Inverness
Country
United Kingdom
Key Stops Applecross Pass (Bealach na Bà), Ullapool, Durness, John o' Groats, Dunrobin Castle
Road Difficulty Long stretches of single-track road with passing places, especially on the west coast, plus a steep hairpin mountain pass at Bealach na Bà
Best Season May–September for weather and daylight; midges are worst in high summer, and some sections are difficult in winter
Recommended Car A standard car is fine; large campervans and motorhomes struggle on the single-track sections

Route des Grandes Alpes
France
↔
Distance About 700 km (435 mi), Lake Geneva (Thonon-les-Bains) to the Mediterranean at Menton/Nice
Country
France
Key Stops Col de la Colombière, Col du Galibier, Col d'Izoard, Col de la Bonette (the highest paved through-road in Europe), Briançon
Road Difficulty A succession of high mountain passes with steep hairpins — well-paved but demanding driving, and many of the cols close completely for winter
Best Season Late June–September, once the highest passes have reopened for the year
Recommended Car A standard car is fine once the passes are open; check each col's status before setting out

Ireland
↔

Transfăgărășan
DN7C · Romania
↔
Distance About 90 km (56 mi) across the Făgăraș Mountains, cresting at Bâlea Lake (2,042 m)
Country
Romania
Key Stops Bâlea Lake, Poenari Castle, Vidraru Dam
Road Difficulty Continuous steep hairpins at high altitude — a genuinely demanding mountain drive, and the road closes completely by snow most winters
Best Season July–September, when the full route is reliably open
Recommended Car A standard car is fine in season; confirm the pass is open before driving up, since it can close with little notice outside summer

Pacific Coast Highway
California State Route 1 · United States
↔
Distance State Route 1 runs about 1,050 km (656 mi) end to end; the classic San Francisco–Los Angeles run through Big Sur is roughly 465 km (290 mi)
Country
United States
Key Stops Big Sur, Hearst Castle, Monterey & Carmel-by-the-Sea, Santa Barbara, Malibu
Road Difficulty Well-paved but narrow and cliffside through Big Sur, with occasional landslide closures that can detour traffic inland for months
Best Season April–October; coastal fog is common on summer mornings and usually clears by midday
Recommended Car A standard car is fine — a convertible is a popular choice for the Big Sur stretch

Carretera Austral
Route 7 · Chile
↔
Distance About 1,240 km (770 mi), Puerto Montt to Villa O'Higgins
Country
Chile
Key Stops Pumalín Park, Queulat hanging glacier, the Marble Caves at Puerto Río Tranquilo, Cerro Castillo
Road Difficulty Long unpaved and gravel sections despite ongoing paving, remote with limited fuel stops, and the route requires at least one ferry crossing (Hornopirén–Caleta Gonzalo)
Best Season November–March, the Southern Hemisphere summer
Recommended Car A high-clearance vehicle or 4x4 is strongly recommended; carry a spare tire and extra fuel

Icefields Parkway
Highway 93 · Canada
↔
Distance 232 km (144 mi), Lake Louise to Jasper
Country
Canada
Key Stops Peyto Lake, Bow Lake, Columbia Icefield & the Athabasca Glacier, Sunwapta Falls
Road Difficulty A well-paved two-lane highway, straightforward in summer; winter driving needs snow tires and caution, and wildlife on the road is a real hazard year-round
Best Season June–September for full access and the clearest weather; open year-round but winter driving is demanding
Recommended Car A standard car in summer; winter tires or a 4x4 are recommended October–April

↔

United States
Key Stops Cadillac Ranch (Amarillo, TX), Petrified Forest National Park, a Santa Fe detour, a Grand Canyon detour, Oatman, Arizona
Road Difficulty A mix of preserved historic alignment and modern highway substitutions — easy, well-paved driving, with the distance and desert heat as the real challenge
Best Season Spring or fall, to avoid summer desert heat in Arizona/New Mexico and winter snow in the Midwest

Asia
Mae Hong Son Loop
Thailand
↔
Distance A roughly 600 km (375 mi) loop from Chiang Mai via Pai and Mae Hong Son, and back
Country
Thailand
Key Stops Pai, Tham Lod Cave, Mae Hong Son town, Su Tong Pae Bridge
Road Difficulty A continuously winding mountain road — popularly said to hold around 1,864 curves — well-paved but demanding to drive alert for the full loop
Best Season November–February, the cool dry season; March–April brings heavy haze from seasonal burning, and June–October is the wet season
Recommended Car A standard car is fine; a smaller car is easier through the tightest curves
