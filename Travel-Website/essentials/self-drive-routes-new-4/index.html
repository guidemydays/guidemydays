Great Self-Drive Routes

Fourteen roads worth renting a car just to drive.

The self-drive routes worth planning a trip around, with the total distance, the stops along the way, how demanding the road actually is, the best season to go, and what kind of car it takes. Booking the vehicle is a separate question — this page is the route itself. Pick the month you're actually going and the roads that suit it come to the top.

Ring Road
Route 1 · Iceland
About 1,320 km (820 mi) — the full loop
Best season, month by month
J F M A M J J A S O N D

From the route’s own note: June–August for reliable roads and long daylight; May and September are quieter with a real chance of snow at higher points

At its bestDrivable, with the caveat aboveThe note warns against itNot named
Country
Iceland
Key Stops
Golden Circle detour, Vík í Mýrdal, Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon & Diamond Beach, Skaftafell, Mývatn, Akureyri, Seyðisfjörður
Road Difficulty
Fully paved and mostly two-lane, but single-lane bridges and blind rises are common. Side trips onto F-road highland tracks need a 4x4 and are only open in summer; ice, wind and short daylight make winter driving demanding.
Recommended Car
A standard 2WD covers the ring road itself in summer; a 4x4 is worth it for any F-road detour or a winter trip

Amalfi Coast Drive
SS163 · Italy
About 50 km (30 mi) between Sorrento/Positano and Vietri sul Mare/Salerno
Best season, month by month
J F M A M J J A S O N D

From the route’s own note: May–June or September–October; many travelers skip driving it in August and take the ferry or bus instead

At its bestDrivable, with the caveat aboveThe note warns against itNot named
Country
Italy
Key Stops
Positano, Praiano, Amalfi, Ravello, Minori, Vietri sul Mare
Road Difficulty
Extremely narrow with continuous hairpin turns along a cliff edge, plus heavy tour-bus traffic in summer — routinely rated one of the more stressful drives in Europe

The smallest car you can rent — width matters more than power on this road
