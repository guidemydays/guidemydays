Go5G Plus: 5 GB high-speed then unlimited 2G (256 kbps) in 215+ countries. Go5G Next: 15 GB high-speed. Note: standard Go5G only covers 11 European countries at high speed — Go5G Plus or above is required for global coverage. Unlimited texting, $0.25/min calls. No daily charge — no action required.

Open T-Mobile Go5G Plus / Next ↗

Charged only on days you use your phone internationally. $12 per 24-hour period for unlimited calls, texts, and up to 2 GB of data at full speed — then reduced speeds for the rest of the day. Additional lines on the same account: $6/day each. Add the pass from the AT&T app before departing — it's not automatic.

Open AT&T International Day Pass ↗

Same model as AT&T — charged per day you use it. 5 GB high-speed then unlimited data at 3G speeds for the remainder of the 24-hour session. Unlimited calls and texts. Enable in the My Verizon app before you leave.

High-speed data included at no extra charge in 200+ countries on the Flexible or Unlimited Premium plans (Unlimited Standard covers only Canada and Mexico free; Unlimited Essentials has no international data). Requires activating and using Fi inside the US for a day first — international data suspends after roughly 80 days away.

Tips

7 entries
Before You Leave

Check that your phone is unlocked before leaving. Most iPhones purchased outright or after 12 months of payments are unlocked. Confirm in Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock (iPhone).

Set up your eSIM before you leave — activation requires a data connection. If you wait until arrival, you'll need airport WiFi to complete it.

Download offline maps before you leave — Google Maps and Apple Maps let you save entire regions. This eliminates data use for navigation and works when you have no signal.

Enable WiFi Calling on your phone (Settings → Phone → WiFi Calling on iPhone). When connected to hotel or airport WiFi, you can make and receive calls on your regular number at no charge.

On the Ground

Enable Airplane Mode immediately after takeoff, then re-enable WiFi only. This prevents accidental roaming charges during the flight and while you set up your eSIM or local SIM on arrival.

On iPhone, go to Settings → Cellular → set your data plan explicitly so the eSIM or local SIM handles data and your home SIM handles calls only. This prevents accidental roaming on your primary number.

WhatsApp, iMessage, Signal, and FaceTime Audio all work over WiFi with no data plan. Use these for messaging and voice calls when you have a WiFi connection.

Buygiffgaff SIM — order free online before you leave (delivered to your home); or Three UK at WHSmith in any major airport

BuyAny EU carrier SIM in your first country — roams free across all EU member states. Orange Holiday (France) is widely recommended: sold at airports and Orange stores.
Gets youOrange Holiday: 20 GB EU-wide, 14 days, ~€25 (~$27). Renew for another 14 days if needed.
NoteFor EU-only trips, Airalo's Europe regional eSIM is often cleaner and comparably priced. Local SIM makes more sense for longer stays. Switzerland and the UK are not in the EU roaming zone — see their own cards.

BuySalt, Swisscom, or Sunrise — kiosks at Zurich, Geneva, or Basel airport arrivals, or any carrier store downtown
Gets youSalt: free SIM + 10 CHF credit, ~1.99 CHF/day unlimited data (~$2); Swisscom/Sunrise: ~20 CHF SIM w/ credit (~$22), ~2–2.5 CHF/day
