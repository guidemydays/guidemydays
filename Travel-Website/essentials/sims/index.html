Phone & SIM Abroad

eSIM, local SIM, or your own carrier, compared honestly.

The three ways to get online abroad, compared honestly: an eSIM set up before you leave, a local SIM bought on arrival, or an add-on from your existing carrier. Each has a case where it is clearly right and a cost range to match. Then the specific providers worth using by region, and the setup details — unlocking, keeping your own number, activation timing — that are easy to get wrong once you have already landed.

US Carrier International Plans

T-Mobile premium plans

Included — 215+ countries
Carrier Included free

Go5G Plus and Experience More 2.0 include 5 GB of high-speed data; Go5G Next and Experience Beyond 2.0 include 15 GB across T-Mobile's worldwide roaming footprint. After that, data continues at up to 256 kbps. Texting is included; calls are $0.50/minute.

AT&T International Day Pass

$12/day when used — 210+ countries
Carrier

Charged only on days you use your phone internationally. For each 24-hour session, eligible plans use their domestic talk, text, and data allowance across AT&T's supported destinations. Additional lines used on the same calendar day are $6 each. Add the pass before departure in the AT&T app or online.

Verizon TravelPass

$12/day when used — 210+ countries
Carrier

Same model as AT&T — charged per day you use it. 5 GB high-speed then unlimited data at 3G speeds for the remainder of the 24-hour session. Unlimited calls and texts. Enable in the My Verizon app before you leave.

Google Fi

Flexible or Unlimited Premium — worldwide coverage
Carrier

Unlimited Premium includes international data across Fi's worldwide coverage; Flexible charges its normal $10/GB rate. Unlimited Standard covers Canada and Mexico, while Unlimited Essentials has no international data. Activate and use Fi in the US first; if most usage is abroad over a consecutive 90-day period, international data can be suspended.

04

Tips

Before You Leave
Check that your phone is unlocked before leaving; eligibility and unlock timing vary by carrier and financing status. On iPhone, confirm that Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock says "No SIM restrictions."
Set up your eSIM before you leave — activation requires a data connection. If you wait until arrival, you'll need airport WiFi to complete it.
Download offline maps before you leave — Google Maps and Apple Maps let you save entire regions. This eliminates data use for navigation and works when you have no signal.
Enable WiFi Calling before departure (Settings → Phone → WiFi Calling on iPhone). Calls still follow your carrier's WiFi-calling rules and may be billed by the number or destination, so check rates before dialing.
On the Ground
Enable Airplane Mode immediately after takeoff, then re-enable WiFi only. This prevents accidental roaming charges during the flight and while you set up your eSIM or local SIM on arrival.
On iPhone, go to Settings → Cellular → set your data plan explicitly so the eSIM or local SIM handles data and your home SIM handles calls only. This prevents accidental roaming on your primary number.
WhatsApp, iMessage, Signal, and FaceTime Audio all work over WiFi with no data plan. Use these for messaging and voice calls when you have a WiFi connection.
