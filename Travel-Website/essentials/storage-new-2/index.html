Luggage Storage

Checkout is 11am, the flight is at 9pm.

Hotel checkout is 11am and the flight is at 9pm — this is where the bags go. What each city's main station actually offers, what the app-based networks charge (Bounce, Radical Storage, Stasher and others), how their per-bag daily rates and insurance cover compare, and the one thing worth knowing about each before you turn up with your bags.

Bounce
Flat rate per bag, per 24 hours
The widest network — 32,000+ partner shops across 4,000+ cities. Around $3–6 per bag per day plus a service fee, with cover up to $10,000 per booking. Long-term storage available.
bounce.com
Radical Storage
Flat rate per bag, per day
13,000+ spots in 2,000+ cities. Roughly €5–6 a day in Europe and from $2.50–4.90 in the US, with no size or weight limit and a €3,000 guarantee for a small protection fee.
radicalstorage.com
Stasher
Rolling 24 hours from drop-off
Strong in the UK, Europe and US cities. From about $2.49 per bag, charged as a flat upfront rate that runs a rolling 24 hours from the moment you drop. Cover up to $10,000 per booking.
stasher.com
LuggageHero
Hourly, with a daily cap
The one to use for short gaps — from about $1–2 per bag per hour, capped at roughly $8 a day. A one-off service fee per bag covers a guarantee up to $3,000. Best value under about five hours.
luggagehero.com
Your hotel
Usually free, same day only
Nearly every hotel holds bags free after checkout and before check-in, and it is almost always the cheapest option. Ask at the desk rather than assuming — and expect same-day only, with no cover if something goes missing.
