Before you go

01

Carry a filtered water bottle (LifeStraw, Brita, GRAYL) in Avoid regions — cuts plastic waste and always gives you clean water.

02

In Avoid regions, brush teeth with bottled water too. The small amount you swallow while brushing is enough to cause issues.

03

Ice at sit-down restaurants in Avoid regions is usually made from purified water. At street stalls, skip it or ask first.

04

Hotels in Avoid regions always provide bottled water. Ask for extra at check-in rather than buying from minibar.
