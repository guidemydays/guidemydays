Train Passes

When the pass wins versus point-to-point fares.

Covers 8 major passes — Eurail Global, Interrail, JR Pass, BritRail, Swiss Travel Pass — what is included, cost and when a pass wins over point-to-point. European operators and routes are covered separately on the European Train Guide. Once you're booked, the Train Station Survival Guide covers the stations that actually cause trouble.

Eurail Global Pass

Coverage Most national rail networks across 33 European countries. Some high-speed and night trains require a paid seat or sleeper reservation even when the journey itself is covered.
Cost range Flexi passes provide a set number of travel days inside a longer validity window; continuous passes cover every day in the chosen period. Check the live fare for your age, class and dates before comparing.
Pass wins when Visiting 3 or more countries with a flexible itinerary and 10+ train journeys in the pass window, or mixing fast trains across borders where advance fares are hard to find.

Interrail Global Pass

Back
Coverage The European-resident counterpart to Eurail, covering participating rail networks across 33 countries. Some high-speed and night trains still require paid reservations.
Cost range Flexi and continuous options are sold in first and second class, with youth and senior discounts on eligible passes. Compare the current pass price plus reservations with advance tickets.
Pass wins when Multi-country, rail-heavy, flexible travel. It is for European citizens or legal residents; travellers who live outside Europe use Eurail instead.

Switzerland Non-residents only
Swiss Travel Pass
Tap for details
Back
Coverage Unlimited travel on the Swiss Travel System rail, bus and boat network, plus included excursions to Rigi, Stanserhorn and Stoos. Most other mountain railways receive a discount rather than free travel.
Cost range In 2026, adult second-class prices are CHF 254 for 3 days, CHF 309 for 4, CHF 399 for 6, CHF 439 for 8 and CHF 499 for 15. The pass also includes admission to more than 500 museums.
Pass wins when Best for several long travel days plus boats, museums or included mountain trips. Price the exact itinerary against point-to-point tickets and the Swiss Half Fare Card before buying.
Buy at sbb.ch
Germany Open to all
Germany Deutschlandticket
Tap for details
Back
Coverage All regional trains (RE, RB, S-Bahn) and local transit (U-Bahn, trams, city buses) across Germany. Does not cover ICE, IC, or EC long-distance trains — those require a separate ticket.
Cost range €63 per calendar month in 2026, sold as a recurring subscription. Visitors can buy it, but must check the seller's payment rules and cancellation deadline; many subscriptions require cancellation by the 10th for month-end.
Pass wins when Slow travel through Germany using regional trains, or a longer city stay with heavy local transit use. Not suited for fast inter-city hops — ICE trains require a separate Bahn ticket regardless.
Buy at bahn.de
Spain Non-residents only
Renfe Spain Pass
Tap for details
Back
Coverage Four, six, eight or ten journeys on Renfe domestic Long Distance trains, including AVE, plus Media Distancia and Avant services. Every journey must be booked against the pass before boarding.
Cost range Standard and Comfort versions are available to travellers who live outside Spain. The first journey can be taken within six months of purchase; after activation, the remaining journeys must be used within one month.
Pass wins when Multiple long-distance trips booked close to departure, especially when flexible fares are high. Advance-purchase point-to-point tickets can still be cheaper, so compare the same trains first.
Check at renfe.com
Single European country Eurail: outside Europe · Interrail: European residents
Eurail / Interrail One Country Pass
Tap for details
Back
Coverage Participating rail networks within one country or defined country group. Available destinations and travel-day options vary; paid reservations still apply on some high-speed and night trains.
Cost range Pricing varies by destination, age, class and number of travel days. Eurail serves travellers who live outside Europe; Interrail serves European citizens and legal residents.
Pass wins when Deep rail exploration of one country with a flexible schedule and several long journeys. Advance fares can undercut the pass, especially once reservation fees are included.
Buy at eurail.com
Japan Non-residents only
Japan Rail Pass (JR Pass)
Tap for details
Back
Coverage Most JR shinkansen, limited express, express and local trains, plus selected JR buses and the JR Miyajima ferry. Nozomi and Mizuho require an additional pass-holder ticket for each trip.
Cost range Ordinary-class passes are ¥50,000 for 7 days, ¥80,000 for 14 and ¥100,000 for 21 through the official online service. Overseas-agency prices rise on October 1, 2026 to ¥53,000, ¥84,000 and ¥105,000; verify the sales channel before paying.
Pass wins when Several long JR journeys within the pass window. It is rarely good value for Tokyo alone; total the exact eligible trips in an official route planner before buying.
Check at japanrailpass.net
United Kingdom Non-residents only
BritRail Pass
Tap for details
Back
Coverage National Rail services in England, Scotland and Wales, including the Elizabeth line, London Overground and Thameslink. It does not cover Northern Ireland, London Underground or private heritage railways.
Cost range Consecutive passes cover 3, 4, 8, 15 or 22 days, or one month. Flexi passes offer 2, 3, 4 or 8 days within one month, or 15 days within two months. Live prices vary by class and age.
Pass wins when Several long trips with spontaneous departure times. It is only for visitors who have not lived in the UK during the previous six months and must be bought before entering Britain; a fixed itinerary may cost less with advance tickets.
Buy at britrail.com
