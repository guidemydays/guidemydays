Transit Cards

Tap on, tap off, no ticket.

A transit card or app lets you tap on and off local buses, metro and trams without buying a ticket each time — load it once and top up as you go. This lists what each city uses, what it covers, whether your contactless bank card works on its own instead, and which cities are app-only, where no physical card exists and you need a digital wallet set up before you arrive.

Did you know The first contactless transit card on Earth wasn't Oyster or Navigo — it was Hong Kong's Octopus card, launched in September 1997. Three million were issued in its first three months, years before any Western city had a tap-in system at all.
