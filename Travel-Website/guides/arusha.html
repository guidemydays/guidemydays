Local Tastes
Nyama Choma
Grilled goat or beef, seasoned simply with salt and chili and served with hot sauce, the anchor of any Arusha night out and Tanzania's default word for a good meal with friends.

Heads Up
Ngorongoro · Manyara · Tarangire — Dawn Departures
Pickup for the three farther parks runs 6:00am - 6:30am to beat the midday heat before wildlife moves into shade.
National Parks — Cashless Entry Only
TANAPA stopped taking cash at park gates; fees are already folded into this guide's day-trip prices, so nothing further is owed.
Worth Knowing

Arusha is a base camp wearing a town's clothes

Every hotel in this guide's price range assumes you are here for the parks, not the town — breakfast starts at 5:30am on safari mornings, and the front desk keeps a shortlist of drivers because everyone eventually needs one. The Clock Tower's "geographical center of Africa, halfway between Cairo and Cape Town" claim is a colonial-era railway fiction — the real midpoint sits deep in the Congo — but it is still where every safari itinerary in town quietly begins and ends. Bring a fleece for the Ngorongoro rim — at nearly 2,300 meters it is genuinely cold before the sun clears the crater wall, even though Arusha itself never asks for one.
