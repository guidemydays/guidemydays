Worth Knowing

Look beyond the marina

Bodrum’s Tuesday market focuses on clothing and textiles. Friday is the classic produce market, with fruit, olives, cheese and seasonal greens; the municipality also lists Thursday produce trading. Holiday weeks can shift the schedule, so check the municipal market page before setting out.

The peninsula’s markets have their own rhythm: Gümüşlük’s produce market is on Wednesday, Bitez’s on Thursday and Turgutreis’s on Saturday. A market visit fits best into the day already taking you to that part of the peninsula.

The gulets in the harbour belong to a working maritime tradition. Bodrum’s boatbuilders and sponge divers helped shape the Blue Voyage: journeys along the coast that later became a defining part of the region’s tourism. A short bay cruise is a taste of that tradition; the classic Blue Voyage continues for several days toward the Gulf of Gökova.
