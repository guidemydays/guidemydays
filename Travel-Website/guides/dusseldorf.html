Worth Knowing

Why Little Tokyo, why Gehry, why the beer feud

Düsseldorf is home to one of Europe's largest Japanese communities, a legacy of Mitsubishi and dozens of other Japanese trading companies choosing the city as their European base after the Second World War. That history is why a city with no obvious Japanese connection has a genuine Little Tokyo around Immermannstraße, with more authentic ramen and sushi counters than almost anywhere else in Germany.

Medienhafen, where Day 1's stops and the hotel itself sit, was a working harbor into the 1970s before falling into disuse and being redeveloped through the 1990s with buildings by Frank Gehry, David Chipperfield and other name architects — the twisting Neuer Zollhof towers are the best-known result.

Düsseldorf and Cologne run a long-running civic rivalry that plays out mostly through beer: Düsseldorf's dark, top-fermented Altbier against Cologne's pale Kölsch, each city convinced its own is correct and the other's barely counts.
