Day 1

1.Hurghada Marina
The modern marina is the easiest first orientation point: a protected promenade of moored boats, cafés and resort traffic where you can settle into Red Sea scale before committing to an offshore day. Walk it in the cooler part of the morning, then use it as your reference point for Sakala.

2.El Mina Mosque
Hurghada’s principal waterfront mosque is a landmark rather than an interior sightseeing appointment. See the pale domes and minarets from the marina side, dress conservatively, and keep clear of prayer times; this is an active place of worship, not a staged attraction.

3.El Dahar Old Town
El Dahar is the older, working end of Hurghada: a useful contrast to the hotel strip, with stalls, everyday shops and a looser street rhythm. Go with a short list, carry small notes, and treat fixed-price shops as the low-stress choice when you are not comfortable bargaining.

Day 2
From Hotel: 15 min → Giftun Islands
1.Giftun Islands
Giftun National Park is the Red Sea day that earns its full-day slot: clear water, reef time and a boat rhythm that cannot be compressed into a city stop. Choose a licensed operator, check wind conditions the evening before, and bring sun protection that can handle long, exposed deck time.

2.Orange Bay
Orange Bay is a beach stop on the Giftun excursion route, with shallow water and a deliberately slow pace between snorkel sessions. It is popular, so the value is in the water and timing rather than solitude; keep your dry bag with you when leaving the boat.

3.Abu Ramada Island
Abu Ramada’s surrounding reefs are a regular snorkel-and-dive zone on Hurghada boat itineraries. Treat the briefing as part of the stop: currents and mooring conditions decide where the boat can safely settle, and reef-safe behavior matters more than adding another swim.

A guided reef stop is where Hurghada’s marine life becomes the point of the trip rather than the view from shore. Float rather than stand, keep fins clear of coral, and skip the water if the crew’s conditions briefing is not reassuring; a responsible short swim beats a rushed long one.

El Gouna is a purpose-built lagoon town north of Hurghada, a distinct day out rather than a different neighborhood. It rewards a slow loop through marina, lagoon and beach settings; arrange the return taxi clearly before you set off because distances after dark are not walkable.

1.Hurghada Desert
A desert excursion gives the itinerary its inland counterpoint: open terrain, heat and a scale that does not read from the coast. Take it with a licensed operator, protect eyes and face from dust, and avoid treating vehicle speed as the attraction; the landscape is the reason to go.

The village stop is usually part of a commercial desert itinerary, so approach it with curiosity and restraint rather than as a claim to an unmediated encounter. Ask your operator what is included, decline animal activities that feel poorly managed, and leave time for the return before dark.

Worth Knowing

Plan boat days around conditions, then use the city as your Red Sea base.

Carry cash for small purchases, agree taxi fares before setting off, and treat reef etiquette as non-negotiable: never stand on coral or feed marine life.

Use a local SIM or hotel reception if a delivery app does not complete foreign-number verification. For water trips, a crew safety briefing outranks a fixed itinerary.
