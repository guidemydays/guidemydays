When to Visit

Spring and autumn are the best seasons to visit. Temperatures are mild, crowds are thinner, and the city's parks are at their most photogenic. Avoid the peak summer months unless you enjoy long queues and midday heat.

The shoulder months of April, May, September, and October balance good weather with reasonable prices. Accommodation is 20–30% cheaper than July and August, and most museums operate without timed-entry restrictions.

Winter visits have their own appeal — fewer tourists, festive markets in December, and the city's architectural details emerge clearly without summer haze. Pack layers; evenings turn cold quickly after sunset.
