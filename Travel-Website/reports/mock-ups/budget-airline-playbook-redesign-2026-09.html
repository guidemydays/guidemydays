The rule: Search the airport IATA code, not the city name, when booking onward transport. "London" can mean six different airports; "LTN" (Luton) tells you exactly which one and which bus you need.

04

Boarding — priority is worth it exactly once.

Priority boarding grants overhead-locker access before economy boards. It matters on full flights where overhead space runs out. On quiet routes, the bus-gate scramble is the same for everyone.

The rule: If you are travelling with only a personal item (under the seat), skip priority boarding — the seat is waiting either way. If you have a carry-on, priority is the only way to guarantee overhead space on a full Ryanair or Wizz flight.
