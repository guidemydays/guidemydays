Concept: "Live Rate Board" — The result is the hero of the page, displayed in 52px type, not a card that appears after filling a form. FROM and TO are large selector cards, not plain text inputs. A scrollable rate strip below shows the FROM currency vs all destinations at a glance. The worth-more table is redesigned as a card grid. Empty state: both cards read "Choose a currency," result side shows "—" — no default pair, no sample conversion, page opens on nothing.
