What it is like

Fast, civilised and priced like an airline — book months ahead or pay dearly, because walk-up fares are brutal. Treat it as a flight at the London end: check-in closes 30–45 minutes before departure and there is airport-style security and passport control. Standard Premier buys a wider seat and a tray of food rather than a different class of travel; the Thalys merger means one booking system and one loyalty scheme across the old network.

What it is like

The best night train in Europe and the reason the whole category came back. The new-generation cars carry single mini-cabins, which finally makes a solo sleeper affordable; couchettes are the value pick and the private sleeper compartments are the ones that sell out first. Book the moment the window opens — popular runs like Vienna–Amsterdam and Vienna–Venice sell out months ahead.

What it is like

A genuinely likeable startup running old, cheap, refurbished stock — this is not Nightjet, and the difference shows in the fittings and the punctuality. Expect delays and a slightly improvised feel. Worth supporting and worth riding if the route suits you, but do not book a tight onward connection off the back of it.
