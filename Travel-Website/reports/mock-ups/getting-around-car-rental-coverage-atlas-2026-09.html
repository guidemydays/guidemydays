Two things that decide the real cost more than the headline rate
1
Where the desk is. Inside the terminal or a shuttle ride away — after a long flight, that walk (or wait) is worth more than a few dollars a day on the rate.
2
What the excess and the hold come to. The insurance excess and the card hold on your deposit vary far more between companies than the day rate, and neither shows up until checkout.
