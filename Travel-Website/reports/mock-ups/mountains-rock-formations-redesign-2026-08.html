Mountains & Rock Formations

Iconic peaks, ridges and formations, and how close you can realistically get to each. The important distinction is between the ones you see from a road or cable car and the ones that need a serious day on foot.

Grand Canyon
UNESCO Site 446 km long · 1.6 km deep
A mile-deep gorge carved by the Colorado River across the Arizona plateau over millions of years. The South Rim is open year-round; the North Rim is seasonal.
