Restaurants & Reservations

Never which site is best — which one they list on.

Restaurant booking is the most stubbornly local corner of travel: a city's restaurants use whichever platform won that market, and the one you use at home is often invisible there.

Multi-Country Booking Country Specialists Guides & Rankings Critics & Crowds Dietary Restrictions
Five things that decide whether you get the table. Book the platform the restaurant actually uses, not the one you know at home. Learn the exact release time — often midnight or 10am local. Card deposits are now standard. Set alerts for cancellations. In southern Europe, Latin America and much of Asia, the answer is often a phone call or WhatsApp — a hotel concierge who calls in the local language succeeds where a form fails.
01
Booking a Table — Multi-Country Platforms

These cover the most ground. Their real advantage over calling is the notification: tell them the date and party size and they alert you when someone cancels.

Resy Reservations

Owned by American Express. Weighted towards chef-driven, newly opened, critically covered restaurants. Deepest in United States cities, growing in London. Some Amex cards unlock tables invisible to others.

United States Canada United Kingdom Europe
OpenTable Reservations

The broadest single network, spanning neighbourhood restaurants and hotel dining rooms. Owned by Booking Holdings, so it also appears inside Booking.com and KAYAK. Useful outside big cities where curated platforms list nothing.

Worldwide
TheFork Reservations · Europe

TripAdvisor-owned, the default across continental Europe (ElTenedor in Spain). Largest listing base in France and Italy. Routinely carries percentage discounts at quieter times — unusual. Also operates in Australia.

France Spain Italy Europe Australia
Tock Prepaid · Tasting menus

Built for restaurants that sell a seat like a theatre ticket. Concentrates the world's tasting-menu restaurants and many wineries. For a destination dinner, it is often the only place the table exists.

United States Europe Worldwide
Google Maps Universal fallback

Not a booking platform, but every restaurant has a listing here — with a Reserve button that hands off to whichever local system it uses. The fastest way to discover that a city runs on a platform you've never heard of.

Worldwide
Quandoo Reservations · Europe · Asia

Berlin-based, strongest in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Lists everyday neighbourhood restaurants the international platforms skip — exactly the places worth eating in on a longer stay.

Germany Austria Switzerland Asia
02
Booking a Table — Country Specialists

In these countries the local platform is not an alternative to the international ones — it is where the restaurants are.

Tabelog Japan · Reviews + booking

Japan's dominant restaurant database. Its ratings are compressed — above 3.5 is genuinely good, above 3.8 exceptional. English site covers a subset; booking often still requires a Japanese phone number.

Japan
TableCheck Japan · English booking

The most visitor-friendly route into Japanese restaurants — English interface, no local phone number required, used by hotel restaurants and mid-to-high-end independents in Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka.

Japan
Pocket Concierge Japan · Fine dining

American Express-owned, aimed at foreign visitors booking Japan's top tables — sushi counters, kaiseki, introduction-only rooms. Prepaid in English. For some of these rooms, the only route in.

Japan
CatchTable South Korea

South Korea's main reservation app, holding most of Seoul's sought-after restaurants. Korean-language, generally expecting a Korean phone number. Naver handles a further slice of bookings.

South Korea
Chope Southeast Asia

The reservation platform of record across Singapore, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta and Bali. In English throughout, accepting foreign cards. Also sells discounted dining vouchers.

Singapore Hong Kong Thailand Indonesia
Dianping Mainland China

China's restaurant review and booking platform, part of Meituan, and the only comprehensive listing. Chinese-language, tied to local payment methods — worth having someone search it for you.

China
EazyDiner India

India's main restaurant reservation platform, covering Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and other major cities, with same-day discounts on a large share of bookings. The platform restaurants use for holding a table.

India United Arab Emirates
Eat App Middle East

Bahrain-founded, the reservation system behind a large share of restaurants across the Gulf — Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Riyadh and Bahrain — in English, accepting foreign cards.

United Arab Emirates Qatar Saudi Arabia Bahrain
CoverManager Spain · Portugal · Latin America

Spanish, the booking engine behind an enormous number of Spanish, Portuguese and Latin American restaurants. You rarely search it directly — you meet it when you click Reservar on a restaurant's page.

Spain Portugal Mexico Latin America
03
Deciding Where to Eat — Guides & Rankings

Each is a different jury. Knowing whose opinion you're reading matters more than the score.

Michelin Guide Anonymous inspectors

Stars for cooking of a high standard. The Bib Gourmand covers good food at a modest price. Coverage is limited to regions it has chosen to enter — unstarred may mean unvisited, not judged and found wanting.

Europe United States Japan Asia
World's 50 Best Restaurants Industry panel vote

Voted by an international panel of chefs, writers and well-travelled diners. It rewards originality rather than consistency, surfacing restaurants Michelin is slower to recognise. Books out the moment a list publishes.

Worldwide
Gault&Millau Europe · Scored out of 20

Scores restaurants out of 20, historically friendlier to modern cooking than Michelin. Strongest in France, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Benelux — frequently rates places the red guide has not reached.

France Germany Austria Benelux
La Liste Aggregated guides

Aggregates hundreds of guides and review sources into a single score, across more than 200 countries. Reaches far more places than any individual guide — useful for a country where none of the famous guides operates.

Worldwide
50 Top Pizza Pizza · Italy & worldwide

An Italian ranking of pizzerias judged by anonymous visits, with world, European and United States editions. The fastest way to avoid tourist-strip pizza in Naples, Rome or New York and find the place locals queue at.

Italy Europe United States
04
Deciding Where to Eat — City Critics & Crowds

Guides rank the top of the market; these cover the rest, which is most of what you will actually eat.

Eater City guides · United States

City-by-city food journalism with local editors. The Eater 38 is an evergreen city-defining list; the heatmap tracks where to eat right now. Written for residents — that's what makes it reliable.

United States United Kingdom
The Infatuation Reviews by occasion

Reviews built around the question people actually ask — where to take a group of six, where to eat alone at the bar. Covers major United States cities plus London. More useful on a trip than any ranked list.

United States United Kingdom
TripAdvisor Crowd reviews · Worldwide

Enormous review volume in every country. Read it for practical detail — whether the terrace is real, whether they take cards, how long the wait was — rather than the ranking, which skews towards tourist-facing restaurants.

Worldwide
Yelp Crowd reviews · United States

The deepest review base for everyday United States restaurants — the diner, the taqueria, the strip-mall place with no website. Coverage outside North America is thin enough to ignore.

United States Canada
Time Out City guides · International

Locally edited food and drink coverage for around forty cities worldwide — including Lisbon, Barcelona, Tokyo, Melbourne, Buenos Aires that Eater and The Infatuation don't reach. Best-restaurant lists are updated, not left to rot.

Worldwide
05
Eating with a Dietary Restriction

A restriction routine at home can be genuinely difficult in a country whose staple dishes are built around it. Carry a printed translation card — kitchens understand it in a way they don't understand a conversation at the pass.

HappyCow Vegan · Vegetarian

A crowd-maintained global directory of vegan and vegetarian restaurants in over 180 countries — including small towns where nothing else lists anything. Vegan/vegetarian/veg-friendly is marked on each entry.

Worldwide
Find Me Gluten Free Coeliac · Gluten-free

Reviews written by people with coeliac disease — discussing dedicated fryers, separate prep areas, and staff understanding. Italy is unusually well served: coeliac diagnosis and certified restaurants are widespread there.

United States Europe Worldwide
