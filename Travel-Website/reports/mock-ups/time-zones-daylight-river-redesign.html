CONCEPT: Daylight River Each city is a horizontal 24h bar. Amber = day, navy = night, gradients = transitions. One scan shows who's asleep while you're awake — the core question of any time-zone page.
HOME BASE LINE A teal vertical line marks the user's own current time across every row simultaneously. At a glance: which cities share your working hours, which are at breakfast, which are asleep.
WOW FACTOR The bars are real-time — the terracotta dot ticks forward live. Hovering a bar shows a tooltip ("3 hrs behind"). The pattern across all cities makes "time difference" visceral, not arithmetic.
