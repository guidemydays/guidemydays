Booking Platforms

Tours & Tickets

The platforms that sell access to what a city is actually doing — guided tours, skip-the-line entry, multi-day trips, concerts and sports. Every major market has its own leaders.

01
You are not buying from them.The operator running the tour is a separate company. When something goes wrong on the day, the operator's own phone is the one that helps — save it from the booking confirmation before you travel.
02
Ratings belong to the product.A 4.9 says nothing about the 4.1 tour next to it. Read the review count too: 4.6 from 3,000 beats 5.0 from nine.
03
Free cancellation is per listing.Two tours on the same platform can have completely different terms. The 24-hour window is measured in local time at the tour location, not yours.
04
The venue's own site is worth one check.For major museums it is often cheaper — but may have no English checkout, no mobile ticket, and no cancellation at all.
Section 01
Tours & Attractions — Worldwide
These four cover most of the planet and compete on the same inventory — the practical difference is price, cancellation terms, and how the voucher works on the day. TripAdvisor owns Viator; the experiences you book on TripAdvisor are Viator's listings.
Worldwide
GetYourGuide Global · Day tours · Skip-the-line
The largest pure-play platform — it exists only for experiences, so product quality and curation are stronger than on travel booking sites that treat tours as an add-on. Strong in Western Europe, Latin America and Southeast Asia. Cancellation terms are usually the clearest of the group.
Visit platform
Viator Global
The largest inventory by count and the one most guides and blogs are affiliated with — which inflates listings without improving quality. Use it when GetYourGuide draws a blank.
Visit platform
Klook Asia-Pacific · Global
Built in Asia and still the dominant platform there — the depth in Tokyo, Seoul, Bangkok, and Hong Kong is meaningfully better than the Western alternatives. Also solid in the Middle East.
Visit platform
Booking.com Attractions Global · Tickets
Attraction tickets and short activities inside the hotel booking flow, weighted towards entry tickets and airport transfers. Convenient when the hotel is already booked there; thinner on guided tours.
Visit platform
Section 02
Regional Specialists
Platforms with a defined geographic focus — stronger inventory and lower prices in their home territory than the global players, and the right answer when the globals draw a blank.
Regional
Airbnb Experiences Global · Small-group · Local hosts
Not a tour marketplace — each experience is sold by the person who runs it, which means quality varies dramatically. The upside: genuinely local knowledge, small groups, and formats no one else sells — cooking in a private home, craft workshops, neighbourhood walks with a local.
Visit platform
KKday Asia-Pacific
The Klook alternative with stronger Taiwan and Japan coverage, and a wider local-experience range. Useful when Klook prices look high or inventory is thin.
Visit platform
Atlas Obscura Worldwide · Unusual & offbeat
Tours and events built around places most platforms don't know exist — underground, abandoned, after-hours, or specialist-led. Useful specifically when you want the unexpected.
Visit platform
Creatrip South Korea
Korean travel platform aimed at international visitors — covers K-pop fan experiences, local restaurants and activities the mainstream platforms rarely list. Accepts foreign cards and ships to overseas addresses.
Visit platform
Section 05
Tickets — Primary Sellers
These sell allocations directly from the venue or promoter — no resale markup, but also no inventory from the other seller's shows.
Primary
Ticketmaster US · UK · Canada · Europe · Australia
The largest primary seller in the world — not a choice but a reality for most major venues, which have an exclusive. Fees are among the highest in the industry, revealed only at the final checkout step. Fan Verified presales require an account linked to the card used at purchase.
Visit platform
NOL World South Korea
The rebranded successor to Interpark Global — South Korea's main ticketing platform for concerts, musicals and sport. Accepts foreign cards and sells a portion of each event to overseas buyers.
Visit platform
See Tickets UK · Europe · US
A large independent primary seller covering UK festivals, theatre and music, with operations in France, Germany, the Netherlands and the US. Holds the allocation for a significant share of British festivals.
Visit platform
