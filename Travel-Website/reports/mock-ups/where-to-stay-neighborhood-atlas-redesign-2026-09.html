Granada

5-day guide

Guide covers Days 1–5 · Spain · Andalusia

Centro & Catedral
Guide 3 4
The cathedral, the Alcaicería market and the Madraza (old Moorish silk exchange) all sit here, walkable from each other. Days 3 and 4 spend the most time in the lower city — markets, the cathedral interior, the Bañuelo baths and the evening tapas circuit north of Gran Vía.
Transit
C30C31 from airport · 15 min walk to all Day 3–4 stops
Best for
Short stays, first-timers, shopping within walking distance
Trade-off
Midday crowds, tourist pricing, noise on Gran Vía
Alhambra Hill & Realejo
Guide 1 5
Day 1 opens on the Alhambra palace and Generalife; Day 5 returns for the Carmen de los Mártires and Fundación Rodríguez-Acosta on the quieter western flank. Hotels here mean walking to the gate rather than queuing for the minibus — a real advantage for the 8:30am timed slot.
Transit
C32 Alhambra Minibus from city centre · 10 min walk to main gate from top stop
Best for
Alhambra-focused trips, early-ticket holders, quiet evenings
Trade-off
Steep terrain, limited dining, hard luggage access
Albaicín
Guide 2
The UNESCO-listed Moorish quarter covers Day 2: the Mirador de San Nicolás, the Carrera del Darro along the river, Hammam Al Ándalus, and a network of cobblestone callejones. Hotels here trade convenience for atmosphere — and the atmosphere is the point.
Transit
C31 from city centre · Very limited car access within the quarter
Best for
Return visitors, architecture lovers, long stays (5+ nights)
Trade-off
No wheeled luggage on cobblestones; no rideshare pickup at many addresses

Part 2

Booking sites, chains & rentals

The district above decides the address. This decides what you book there and on which channel — and the same room can carry a different price on each one.

5–15%
No two booking sites show the same price for the same room. Search one broad site to see what exists, then check the winner on a second before paying.
Chain

A chain sells predictability. The room is the size the photos showed, there is a staffed desk at 2am, the address is one every taxi driver knows, and luggage can be left before check-in and after checkout. That predictability earns its keep on short trips, late arrivals, unfamiliar cities, travel with children, and anything work-related where the hotel is not supposed to be the story.

Boutique

A boutique hotel sells the opposite — usually a building with a history, in an old centre where no chain could get planning permission. The rooms differ from one another, breakfast is cooked rather than laid out, and the person at the desk books your table personally. The trade is variance: service depends on who is on shift, and there is nobody above the owner to escalate to.

Search & booking platforms
Booking.com
Search

The widest inventory of any single platform — independent hotels, apartments, hostels and chains in one search. Free cancellation policies are prominently filtered. Genius loyalty discounts trigger from level 2 onward. Use it to establish what exists in the district, then cross-check the winning option elsewhere.

booking.com
Expedia
Search

Most useful when bundling hotel with a flight — the package discount is real and often exceeds the Expedia One loyalty points value. Standalone hotel search is competitive but rarely cheaper than Booking on identical rooms.

Rental platforms
Airbnb
Rental

Best for groups of three or more, stays of a week or longer, or cities where chain hotels don't reach residential neighbourhoods. Cleaning fees and service charges add 20–40% to the nightly rate; compare the total before assuming a win.

airbnb.com
HomeToGo
Rental

An aggregator that cross-searches Airbnb, Booking, Vrbo, and independent rental agencies in one query. Useful when Airbnb's inventory feels thin — particularly in Andalusia and Bavaria, where local rental agencies list on their own platforms rather than centralising on Airbnb.
